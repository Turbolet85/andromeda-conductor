- test-plan §7 (Seed strategies → Golden artifacts) requires insta goldens committed and run **CI fail-don't-write, never `cargo insta review`**; stream goldens are one file per test-file family × seed (`replay__*` · `pacing__*` · `dispatch_wire__*`) with no wall-clock field to redact (the dispatch projection excludes every `*_time_unix_nano`).
- test-plan §10 requires the workspace gates this chunk must pass: line coverage ≥ 60% (`--fail-under-lines 60`), a **zero-flakiness budget with nextest `retries` unset**, and `cargo audit --deny warnings` + `cargo deny check` green over a committed un-drifted `Cargo.lock` (§11 §CI restates the merge ban).

- Harvest-tier assertion at the **`conductor-run` unit tier** over verbatim Pulse tracing captures — test-plan §6 records this shape for both prior families (`storm_harvest.rs` for `triage.pattern.storm.detected`; `baseline_harvest.rs` for `triage.cue.emit` kind/priority/magnitude/confidence/`suppression_bypassed`). - Envelope readout per test-plan §3 `status`/`logs`: bound-parameter `SELECT` of the `runs.db` row and/or `serde_json::from_str::<RunReportEnvelope>` / `jq -e` over `runs/<run_id>.jsonl` — the journal is the SLO ground truth and the left side of every latency check.
- Table-driven fixtures per test-plan §4/§7: rstest `#[fixture]` seeded `conductor-timeline` generator + `#[rstest]`/`#[case]` rows; scenario config stays a committed declarative serde+garde file under `scenarios/` (a re-shape re-enters the §1 scenario-config validation surface: bounded `error_percent` ∈ 0..=100, bounded per-phase occurrences, p50≤p95≤p99, per-shape emission rules).
- Golden discipline split per test-plan §7 + §4 (conductor-report bullet): exact-string `assert_eq!` for canonical unit-tier serialization goldens; insta with `run_id`/timestamp redaction for the E2E journal goldens.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- test-plan §11 §E2E: **NEVER `sleep(N)` for synchronization** — wait on an explicit signal (envelope `state`, a JSONL journal line, exit code); and never treat a reported state as a hard-fail exit.
- test-plan §11 §Test Data (stack-specific): **NEVER stamp the emission journal/report from tokio's virtual clock** — `journal_emitted_at` / `latency_ms` come from `std::time::SystemTime`/`Instant`; a paused-clock leak corrupts the SLO basis this chunk re-measures for the `slo_tier` re-declaration.
- test-plan §11 §Quality + §CI: **NEVER lower the coverage bar, add retries, or skip the audit/deny gate "just this once"** — which is also why the chunk's audit PREREQ must be re-pinned on a recorded basis rather than muted via a `deny.toml` ignore or a CI edit.
