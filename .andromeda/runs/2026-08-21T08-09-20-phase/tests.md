## Anti-patterns to avoid
- §11 (Test Strategy, stack-specific): never fake Pulse's reaction as a CI verdict — the stub canary proves MCP wiring only. Corollary from §6's fingerprint-storm record: a green resting on an `Absent` check against a surface that cannot carry the token is a vacuous pass, and the plan's remedy is measured retirement, not a retained green.
- §11 (E2E): never `sleep(N)` for synchronization — wait on an explicit signal (envelope `state`, a JSONL journal line, exit code). Directly binding on a lifecycle scenario whose observable is a 120s-idle transition.
- §11 (Integration, stack-specific): never rely on nextest's per-test process to isolate a test from the process-global first-install-wins `init_observability` subscriber — a new harvest test needs its own `tests/*.rs` binary, never `--test-threads=1` serialization.
