## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER introduce an inbound listener of Conductor's own or bind `:4317` in a test — the port-occupier is the sole deliberate bind and must release on cleanup; loopback stubs on ephemeral ports only (per test-plan.md §11 → Universal, project-specific; §3 → `cleanup` Verification).
- NEVER let a stub answer stand in for Pulse's reaction as a CI verdict — a stub canary proves wiring only; and NEVER manufacture a hard-`Fail` exit from a reported state (per test-plan.md §11 → Test Strategy, stack-specific; §11 → E2E, stack-specific).
- NEVER synchronize a probe/timeout test with `sleep(N)` — wait on an explicit signal (exit code, envelope field, journal line); a flake is quarantined at the cause, and `retries` stay 0 (per test-plan.md §11 → E2E; §10 Quality Gates → Zero-flakiness budget).
