## Patterns to follow
- One-spawn-site per-suite isolation: the routine arm re-seeds its fixture dir CLEAN per session, the driven arm persists under its own `CONDUCTOR_RUNS_DIR=runs/driven/runs`, and the `sr*` suites pick their runs/scenarios dirs at the single spawn site — so no session's journal leaks into another's subject (per test-plan.md §6).
- Subject seeding through the production writer: `seedFixtureRuns()` invokes the `conductor-run` `envelope_fixture` target under `CONDUCTOR_E2E_SEED_DIR` and THROWS on a non-zero exit or missing journal, rather than copying a file (per test-plan.md §3 CI stage selectors · §7 provenance SET).
- Meaning-under-test round-trip for any committed fixture — one production reader/writer round-trip per fixture, the pattern set by `lamps_fixture.rs`, `envelope_fixture.rs` and `commands::tests::the_committed_scenarios_fixture_stays_loadable` (per test-plan.md §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement).
- Render-independent half beside the rendered half: a claim asserted at the unit / mock-runtime tier as well as in the DOM (the `--status-residual` pair is the shipped precedent) (per test-plan.md §6 routine-arm paragraph).
- Selector discipline for any markup change: role / text / `aria-live` + brand anchors — the closed six lamp labels, the non-lamp caption SET, `role="alertdialog"`, the "HOLD — operator pause" heading text, mono P-ID tokens (per test-plan.md §6 Selector strategy).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- No xpath, no hashed Tailwind/CSS-class selectors, and no `sleep(N)` for synchronization — wait on an explicit signal (envelope `state`, a journal line, an element role/`aria-live`, an exit code) (per test-plan.md §11 E2E).
- No manual smoke step and no "human reviews screenshots" arm; the only sanctioned human step is the `drive+observe` operator checklist, so a browse-class row may not be converted into a manual verification to make finding 7 gradeable (per test-plan.md §11 Universal · §1 Untestable zones).
- Never run a live-Pulse leg as a CI gate, and never let a context-skip (or a `CONDUCTOR_NVDA`-unset skip-at-exit-0) read as a pass (per test-plan.md §11 CI · §6 routine-arm rule).
