- **Hierarchy depth:** 2 levels (epoch → chunks)
- **Reasoning:** Zero new surfaces against 9 pre-existing crates and 22 unchanged Established Decisions puts this below the surface × crate band that warrants sub-blocks — the 10 capabilities are four independent retirement tracks, each small enough that an intermediate sub-block layer would be an artificial tier over 2–4 leaves.

### Adjustments applied (optional)

No adjustments — base heuristic applies. Each optional bump was checked against a concrete signal and none fired: security tier is `Minimal (0)`, not Hardened (security-plan §Threat Model Summary); arch carries no v2/v3 deferred-feature markers for this version; the host is Windows-only single-platform, not multi-platform native; and input.md's rigor signals are the 0.1.0 brief's, already discharged, adding nothing this version must carry.

## Notes for Phase 1 synthesis

Cross-epoch dependencies and sequencing constraints identified during calibration:

- **Epoch 1 → Epoch 3 is a genuine data dependency, not a preference.** `v3-02`'s terminal fork is *decided by* `v3-01`'s reading: a probe that finds an actionable cause routes to "gate runs green", one that finds an immovable image property routes to "ratified permanent exclusion". Phase 1 must not place the terminal chunk as if it could be authored before the probe returns, and must not pre-commit the route to either branch.
- **Epoch 1 → Epoch 4's blocked entry.** `v3-07` (state the contract) is unblocked and lands in Foundation; `v3-08` (re-drive and grade) is `BLOCKED-ON` a Pulse-side emission. Per this project's standing rule, that annotation states the measurable EVENT that clears it — Pulse emitting the contracted observable — never a standing state, and the entry carries its measurement pointer inline.
- **Epoch 2's chunks are class-scoped by the intent's own ruling.** "As a class in one pass" is explicit: retiring the `constellation-severity-live-wiring` member alone would contradict an accepted sibling already ratified at `architecture-amendments.md:403`. Phase 1 must not decompose `v3-04` into per-scenario chunks.
- **Epoch 2 carries a false-modify-set hazard worth naming in the chunk text.** The pinning tests are the paired half of every retirement, and one lives in an inline `#[cfg(test)]` module under `src/` that the prescribed sweep cannot reach.
- **Epoch 4's live legs need serialization, not just ordering.** Measured during 0.2.0's in-lane round: Pulse dedupes a new incident against ANY open incident, and every `conductor run` fires its own preflight canary — so back-to-back legs land `Blocked` rows. Each live leg rides its own quiet window; Phase 1 should keep live-drive chunks separate rather than bundling several legs into one.
- **Epoch 4's grading posture is stated before the drive, by design.** `v3-09`'s whole point is that non-determinism is handled as a declared property. A chunk that drives first and decides the grading rule afterwards would satisfy the letter and defeat the capability.
