- **Indented-detail-line precedent** (§cli Component — Primary content block 2, per-check detail region; the `Blocked` precondition string and `KnownResidual` note) — the established way to add per-grain detail with no new column, no new bracket label, no new ANSI entry.

- Re-pinning a fresh derived literal in place of a stale one, in any doc or sample this work touches (§cli Primary screens, `conductor coverage` roll-up caption; reinforced by the founder-approved de-literalization ruling in the amendment history below).
