## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Never convert a dead check into a vacuous one: §6 records the always-failing `Contains` and the VACUOUSLY-passing `Absent` as the *same* defect class — retirement is the remedy, polarity-flipping is not (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm).
- Never re-express a retired check as a hard failure, and never treat `KnownResidual` / `CalibrationRegion` / `blocked` as a non-zero process exit (per test-plan §11 E2E, stack-specific).
- Never carry the edited suite with a gate concession — no lowered `--fail-under-lines`, no nextest `retries`, no skipped gate; and never drop a scenario's P-ID while retiring its checks (per test-plan §11 Quality · §11 CI · §11 Universal project-specific).
