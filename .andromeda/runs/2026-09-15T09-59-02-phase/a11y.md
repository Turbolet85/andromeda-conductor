Any test-scaffolding this chunk adds stays in the Rust inline `#[cfg(test)]` module, not in the webview harness.
- **Arm separation.** a11y-plan §9 splits the routine arm (no live Pulse; seeds its own committed fixture through the production writer) from the operator-only `driven` and `sr*` arms (live Pulse / NVDA required). The chunk declares itself "Not a live Pulse drive", so the driven and `sr*` arms are not its carrier — consistent with the split.
