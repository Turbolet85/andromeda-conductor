## Relevance
Relevant — the chunk edits an assertion inside the routine a11y `--e2e` arm and turns on that arm's printed CI verdict, both of which are test-plan-owned surfaces (§3 `--e2e` stage selector, §6 desktop-webview drivers, §9 E2E-webview stage row).

## Constraints
- The `--e2e` leg's verdict must be read from the **printed verdict, not the exit code** (the handle guard is designed to skip at exit 0), requiring three predicates: zero on the `Spec Files:` failed count, a per-spec skip tally **within the expected-skip SET** (the two live-hold subjects; a third skip is a failure), and the `[webview2 … windows]` driven-session banner — per test-plan §3 (`run` → CI stage selectors, `--e2e`). This bears directly on the scope's open question 4: §3 mandates a grading on the *failed count* and the *skip SET*, and names no pass-tally predicate, so a move from 11/1/2 to 12/0/2 touches only a quantity the contract does not read. Whether the shipped `Assert-A11yVerdict` implements that form (vs. a baked `$A11yExpectedSkips` literal) is research's question.
- A tally that grows with the suite must be carried as the **SET it evidences, never a baked literal** — §6 Drivers per surface records the arm's pass tally as a moving count with the SET as what the sample evidences, and §6's static-gate Verification signal forbids the literal-count predicate form outright. The corrected `:384` and any doc/verdict text it touches are governed by this.
- **A runner-dependent result is a determinism break to fix at the cause**, never a runner to pin to, and flaky tests are quarantined-and-fixed with nextest `retries` held at 0 (per test-plan §10 Zero-flakiness budget; §11 CI). Scope's open question 2 (why the cycle wraps on the runner and not the dev host) sits in this class: a correction that absorbs the environment difference instead of removing it is the banned shape.
- E2E assertions use **role / text / `aria-live` / brand-anchor selectors only** — no xpath, no hashed CSS-class selectors — and may not `sleep(N)` to synchronise inside a test; wait on an explicit signal (per test-plan §11 E2E; §6 Selector strategy).
- The a11y **gate step never carries `continue-on-error`**, since a gate that can report success by skipping or erroring is what the strict arm (`CONDUCTOR_A11Y_STRICT`) exists to forbid; and a **context-skip is never a pass** (per test-plan §9 E2E-webview stage row; §6 Drivers per surface, routine arm).
- The routine arm is the **unattended, no-live-Pulse family**; live-Pulse legs (`a11y:driven`, `sr*`, `run --live`) are operator/local only and never a CI gate (per test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios; §11 CI ban). Nothing in this chunk may move a live leg into the `a11y` job.
- The frontend carries **no JS/TS unit runner**, and the build gate (`tsc --noEmit` + `vite build`) does not prove a wdio member even loads — a member is proven only by EXECUTING the leg that loads it (per test-plan §4, `conductor-tauri/ui` bullet). So the corrected assertion's own correctness has no cheaper prover than the arm itself.

## Patterns to follow
- **Both-directions exact-set equality with no literal-count predicate** — the shape §6 mandates for the coverage-completeness gate and its scenario-backing / scenario-assertion-audit sibling legs (`unpinned` / `rotted` / `lost_subject`, each error naming the offending member). This is the established in-repo shape for "assert the property, not the tally", which is exactly what the `:384` counting-basis correction is reaching for.
- **De-literalization precedent** — §6's baked `10 passing / 2 skipped` sample was replaced by the SET it evidences rather than by a fresher count (per the 2026-09-07-sr-findings-fixed amendment); follow that when recording this arm's new tallies anywhere.
- **The routine arm seeds its own subject before the session**, through the production writer into a dedicated repo-relative runs dir, throwing rather than seeding silently (per test-plan §3 `--e2e`; §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement). The idle-console DOM SC 2.1.1 walks is that seeded subject's render.
- **Committed fixtures carry MEANING, not just parseability** — each has a production reader/writer round-trip at the Rust tier so a semantically inert change fails at `cargo nextest` rather than silently weakening a green webview assertion (per test-plan §7). Relevant if the correction leans on the fixture's control set.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Never substitute a fresh literal for a set property (a new count re-stales at the next control added to the console) — per test-plan §6 (static-gate Verification signal) and the §6 Drivers-per-surface moving-tally rule.
- Never obtain the green by a skip, a `continue-on-error` on the gate step, or a retry budget — per test-plan §9 (E2E-webview row), §6 (context-skip is never a pass), §10 (Zero-flakiness budget).
- Never reach for xpath / hashed-class selectors or a `sleep(N)` synchronisation when re-basing how the Tab cycle is observed — per test-plan §11 E2E.

## Contract bindings
- **tests ↔ a11y:** what SC 2.1.1 / SC 2.4.3 must assert is a11y-plan's call; how the arm is driven, graded and CI-gated is test-plan's (§3 `--e2e`, §6 routine-arm registration, §9 E2E row). The a11y CI gate is the binding point named in the tests focus guide.
- **tests ↔ obs:** the arm emits one violation record per run at `runs/a11y/<run_id>.jsonl` — the §3 envelope's eleven fields PLUS obs §9 resource tags, deliberately outside the harness's non-recursive `runs/*.jsonl` glob so `status` cannot read it as a scenario run — and the job reuses the `journal_conformance` gate over `runs/a11y` (per test-plan §3 `--e2e`, §9 E2E row). Any job-content change must leave §3 ↔ obs-plan §3 in agreement.
- **tests ↔ security / arrangement:** escalations 1, 3 and 4 in the scope gate what the `a11y` job may contain; test-plan §9's stage row is where the job's step set, its launch path and the never-`continue-on-error` gate rule are recorded, so an operator ruling that changes the job changes that row too.

## Acceptance criteria contributions
- (tests) The routine arm's **printed verdict** reads green on the measured configuration: zero on the `Spec Files:` failed count, per-spec skip tally within the expected-skip SET (the two live-hold subjects — a third skip is a failure), and the `[webview2 … windows]` banner present (per test-plan §3, `run` → CI stage selectors, `--e2e`).
- (tests) The corrected SC 2.1.1 assertion grades a **set property by both-directions comparison** and carries **no literal-count predicate and no baked pass tally** (per test-plan §6, Coverage-matrix completeness gate Verification signal + §6 Drivers per surface).
- (tests) The correction is proven by **executing the wdio leg that loads it** — the build gate does not prove it and no JS/TS unit runner exists (per test-plan §4, `conductor-tauri/ui` bullet).
- (tests) The green is not obtained by a skip, by `continue-on-error` on the GATE step, or by a retry, and the arm's result is not runner-pinned (per test-plan §9 E2E-webview row + §10 Zero-flakiness budget).
