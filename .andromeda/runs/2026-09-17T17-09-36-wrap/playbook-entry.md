- pattern: a VERBATIM DUPLICATION inside a spec master's own body — the same clause stated twice within one line or in
    adjacent lines, with NO semantic delta between the two — repaired in place by deleting one occurrence
  verdict: routine
  note: |
    apply — a pure text repair with no judgment content. No claim changes, nothing is retired, no mechanism is
    restated, and the body is strictly closer to its own intent afterwards; the only thing a reader loses is the
    stutter. Scoped tightly, and each clause is a precondition: the two occurrences must be BYTE-IDENTICAL (a
    near-duplicate that differs in wording may be a deliberate restatement in a different register — that is a
    judgment call and stays escalate), and the repair deletes ONE occurrence without rewriting either. This rule
    does NOT license removing prose a reader merely judges redundant, condensing a passage, or merging two
    sentences that say similar things differently — those change what the body asserts and remain outside it.

    because the class had NO routine channel: a11y-plan §1's dittography was found by a semantic read, no detector
    covers it (a line-granular grep shows one hit and cannot see a clause standing twice inside one line), and it
    therefore rode a working-route CARRY as an operator directive from the 2026-09-12 wrap until this chunk — five
    days and one re-measurement of its offsets, for a 72-character deletion. Two practical notes for the next one:
    locate the duplication BY OFFSET within the line and carry the offsets rather than a line number, since any
    amendment to the same line moves them; and re-measure them immediately before the edit, never trusting the
    carried pair.
