- 2026-09-23: **Pulse logs NO line when its model DISMISSES a digest** — a `Dismiss`, a severity-`None` and a resolution-summary output all return silently from incident creation — so a real-model leg's missing incident cannot be read from an absence of errors: the witness is `interpretation.json.parse` `ok` for that digest with NO `interpretation.incident.created` after it, and `digest.runtime.cadence_tick`'s `cue_present` is what separates the one cue-bearing digest that could have formed the incident from the cue-less baseline digests around it. Classify a real-model `Blocked` as MODEL-SIDE only on that pair; a missing parse line or a failed inference request is a pipeline fault instead, and only that kind may be re-fired.
