## Constraints
- The `a11y` job must stay a fail-fast gate. a11y-plan §11 CI ("NEVER skip a11y on CI/operator-gate failure") requires `CONDUCTOR_A11Y_STRICT` and the printed-verdict assertions to keep a skipped-green run from passing. New steps or jobs in `ci.yml` must not weaken, reorder or condition that job. Whether a new workflow-level or job-level change reaches that job is research's question.
- Refer to CI jobs by NAME, not by `ci.yml` line numbers (per a11y-plan §3 Configuration). Adding steps will shift the `:61-72` / `:354` coordinates the scope cites.
- The `a11y` job is green only on one measured configuration (per a11y-plan §11 Strategy and §9 Platform): hosted `windows-2022`, a matching 131.0.2903.86 driver/runtime pair, High integrity, and the driver pinned from the image's own runtime. The chunk must not move any of these. The msedgedriver pin is the one allowed CI-time fetch the scope's item 4 counts. A scanner fetched at job time would add a second fetch, and it must not share or change the a11y job's driver fetch.
- a11y-plan §9 CI-specific resource attributes requires a11y artifacts to be tagged `service.name`, `deployment.environment`, `ci.run.id` and `git.commit.sha` "set via CI env". The a11y job also uses `CONDUCTOR_A11Y_STRICT` (per a11y-plan §11 CI) and `CONDUCTOR_MSEDGEDRIVER` (per a11y-plan §3 Bootstrap `a11y-ci-gate-wire`). The undeclared-env-key check (scope item 6) must treat these as declared. Research should answer two things: whether the job references any of them through the `env` context, and whether its driver pin writes any key to `GITHUB_ENV` (scope item 8).
- a11y artifacts must not leak absolute host paths (per a11y-plan §3 Structured violation JSON schema and §11 SLO). Tracked a11y evidence also holds hex-shaped content: `fingerprints[]` tuples, `evidence/nvda-pass.json`, and run ids from runs such as 35208593666. These belong in the false-positive baseline (scope item 5), and any allowlist entry for them needs a stated reason. Whether these files currently match the chosen patterns is research's question.

## Patterns to follow
- A strict mode plus a check of the printed verdict (per a11y-plan §11 CI). The gate should fail loudly, and a pass should be distinguishable from a skip, not judged on exit code alone. This is the same need as the scanner's "seeded known-bad input to prove it can fail".
- Name every exception in a list and have a checker enforce it (per a11y-plan §11 Strategy: `claim-ownership.ts` plus `npm run a11y:ownership`, which fails if an entry points at nothing). This is a model for an allowlist where every entry carries a reason and a stale entry is caught.
- State CI results together with their configuration, not as a bare "green" (per a11y-plan §11 Strategy and §9 Platform). The new gate's claimed green should record the runner and the commit it was measured at.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Do not put `continue-on-error`, a soft or warning mode, or a retry on any gate that shares the workflow with the a11y job (per a11y-plan §11 CI and §11 SLO "NEVER use a retry-once policy" / "NEVER define a soft a11y SLO").
- Do not add a CI network fetch to, or change the driver-pin step of, the `a11y` job (per a11y-plan §9 Platform, configuration-bound green).
