A pre-stated series of at most five drives. Each drive is fired through `bash scripts/agent-run.sh run --live real-model` against a pulse-app launched per `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md`, and the overseer confirms the host is free before each one. The series design, its decision rule, the grading rules and this pass condition are all fixed in that contract before the first drive. Every drive is recorded: its capture is committed under the chunk's `evidence/`, pinned byte-equal by `crates/conductor-run/tests/real_model_harvest.rs`, and logged as one row in `evidence/attempt-ledger.md`.

A drive whose preflight canary forms no incident is a canary measurement. It is recorded with its classification and never graded. A drive whose scenario emitted and was read back is graded.

The capability is met iff at least one drive is graded AND every graded drive's pinned outcome is `Identified` under the unchanged rule: the attributed incident's rank-1 hypothesis statement names `conductor` as a whole word and a retry token (`retry` / `retries` / `retrying`), case-insensitively. The real-model witnesses must also hold for each graded drive: Pulse's `interpretation.model.load` reads `inference_mode = real`, and the attributed report carries no `det-` evidence ref. A separate ref test, written only when this condition holds, asserts both over the committed captures. A graded `NotIdentified` outcome means NOT met; it is recorded, and no further drive replaces it (no retry-until-pass).

Stated limit, unchanged: the cue line reaches the model verbatim and itself satisfies the rule, so `Identified` means the real model carried the cue's scope and kind into rank 1. It is not inference of an unstated cause.
