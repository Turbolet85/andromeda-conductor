## Patterns to follow
- A gate IS its Rust test. CI steps invoke the named nextest targets (`-p conductor-report --test coverage_gate`, `-p conductor-core --test scenario_audit_gate`) behind a presence guard, so this chunk re-lists no assertion in shell (per architecture §Established Decisions [CI/CD]).
- A declare-only scenario with zero `[[expected]]` checks grades its claims at the harvest tier. `real-model-interpretation` is the precedent: declare-only from birth, not a tenth family, and a non-degraded success row lands `ManualCheck` (per architecture §Standard Contracts — Run report envelope).
- Scenario config is TOML deserialized with serde and checked with garde, with membership checked against the manifest by `Scenario::from_toml_str_with` at every catalog load site. A sibling-spanning rule ships as a `Scenario::check_*()` load-path method, never as `#[garde(custom)]` (per architecture §Established Decisions [Validation Library] · [Scenario Config Format]; §Conventions — Config conventions).
- The harness shells own the capture files. The real-model arm writes and clears exactly `rm.jsonl` / `rm-capture.txt` / `rm-capture.err` under `runs/live-suite/`, non-recursively, and strips `RUST_LOG` for its two test invocations (per architecture §Occupied Resources — `runs/live-suite/{leg}.jsonl` · `RUST_LOG`).
- A SUT coordinate is a transcribed record carrying its HEAD. The plan records Pulse readings at `83d4060`, so any cited Pulse path (the `create_incident_from_l4_output` early return, the `inference_runtime.rs` dedupe and creation lines) is re-read at `f15536b` before being restated (per architecture §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture]; §Occupied Resources — `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md` provenance).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Widening the canary block into a silent pass or a generic corpus-empty state. A model-side non-surfacing is `NoAttributableIncident → Blocked`. The staleness block means "this run's storm raised nothing", never "the wiring is broken". A Pulse-side cause (its prompt, thresholds or creation predicate) is not Conductor's to build (per architecture §Standard Contracts — Readiness gate; §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture]).
- Adding a new `contracts/` member reader, `CONDUCTOR_*` handle, `agent-run` verb or OTLP service identity without registering it. §Occupied Resources is the registry, and its size is checked by `scripts/arch-registry-check.py` against 60 % of the Read cap (per architecture §Occupied Resources; §Stack and Technologies — Operator instruments).
- Reading the canary's `tracked_fingerprints_count` as evidence of absence. It is a 60 s windowed gauge sampled after eviction; use the cumulative `storms_detected_total` / `fingerprints_evicted_total` (per architecture §Occupied Resources — `contracts/pulse-run-contract.toml`).
