## Anti-patterns to avoid

Journal-relative latency measured the whole emission window live (18169 / 35120 / 30212 ms against sidecar calls of 0–22 ms), so it is the wrong quantity (per obs-plan §4 Delegated-timing family; §5 Per-surface / per-path metrics).
- Minting a span name outside the bounded set (`scenario.run`, `timeline.execute*`, `emit.batch`, `emit.logs_batch`, `verify.readback*`, `report.generate`, `db.insert_run`, `fault.*`, `tauri.command.*`), or a span attribute outside the allowlist, to carry hue evidence. The processor stage drops it silently (per obs-plan §11 Spans / Traces; §4 Known-residual Required span attributes).
- Committing a capture fixture of Pulse's log lines that still holds an absolute host path. The enumerated redaction sites never bound every host-path channel, and a committed third-party capture is one such channel (per obs-plan §11 Logs; §11 PII Scrubbing, "NEVER assume the three application sites above bound every host-path channel").
