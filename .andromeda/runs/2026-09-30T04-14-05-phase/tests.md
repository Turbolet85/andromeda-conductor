## Relevance
relevant — three of the five items are test-surface work: the harvest's pin mechanism (items 1 and 3), the capture's mask arm (item 2), and the flaky a11y CI gate (item 4). The drive series (item 5) falls under the plan's operator-gated live-leg discipline.

## Constraints
- The real-model interpretation claim grades at the HARVEST tier in `crates/conductor-run/tests/real_model_harvest.rs` in the DEFAULT suite, over the drive's scrubbed capture. The plan also requires two further things. Synthetic arms must cover every outcome plus the extraction, token-surface and host-path-mask arms. Every committed capture must be "pinned byte-equal to its section" (per test-plan §6 Fingerprint-storm → Real-model interpretation leg). Replacing the literal pins with sha256 digests changes the MECHANISM behind that sentence. Whether a digest match discharges "byte-equal" as §6 words it, or whether §6 needs a wrap amendment, is a question for research and wrap. The masters stay read-only in this chunk.
- A committed fixture must carry its MEANING under test, not just its parseability (per test-plan §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement). A digest pin proves identity only. The grading, routing and witness assertions the literals feed today must therefore keep running over the file's CONTENT. Whether the harvest already reads committed evidence files at test time is research's question.
- The suite must pass under BOTH `cargo nextest` and `cargo test -p <crate>`. A result that depends on the runner falls in the zero-flakiness class and must be fixed at its cause (per test-plan §4 Framework; §10 Zero-flakiness budget). A test-time read of a committed file must resolve the workspace root from `CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR`, never from a CWD-relative path, because a cargo test binary's CWD is its package root (per test-plan §6 Coverage-matrix completeness gate, the `check_sut_drift` arm's loading rule).
- `real_model_live.rs` sits behind the empty `live-pulse` feature. A gated file is invisible to the default lint pass, so any change to it owes its own `cargo clippy -p conductor-run --features live-pulse --all-targets -- -D warnings` (per test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios). It is a FIFTH-class test-binary reader: it reads the `CONDUCTOR_RUNS_DIR` handle and the `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_DATA_DIR` value through the shared `capture_paths` guard, whose mandated negative test is the default-suite target `capture_paths_guard` (per test-plan §1 Coverage triggers, security-vector-coverage Vector 1). A workspace-key mask derived from the data dir's basename must read that value through the guard, never around it.
- Flakes are not tolerated. A flake is quarantined and fixed at its ROOT CAUSE; nextest `retries` stays 0, and no retry-once policy may be added anywhere (per test-plan §10 Zero-flakiness budget; §11 Quality). The `a11y` job's GATE step never carries `continue-on-error`, because a gate that can report success by skipping or erroring is exactly what the strict arm forbids (per test-plan §9 Pipeline structure, E2E (webview) row).
- `--e2e` passes on the PRINTED VERDICT, not on the exit code. Three things are required: zero on the `Spec Files:` failed count, a skip tally inside the expected-skip SET (the two live-hold subjects), and the `[webview2 … windows]` banner (per test-plan §3 `run` → CI stage selectors). A TS e2e member is proven only by EXECUTING a leg that loads it; the build gate does not prove it (per test-plan §4 What unit tests cover → conductor-tauri/ui). An edit to `wdio.conf.ts` or `accessibility.e2e.ts` is therefore proven by the `--e2e` leg.
- `run --live real-model` is operator-gated and never a CI stage. It is driven only as the pre-stated series of `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md`, fixed before the series' first drive. Every drive is recorded and never replaced. Only a Conductor-side environment or pipeline fault may be re-fired, and only once. Each drive's `rule_record` goes into `rm-capture.txt` BEFORE the leg, and a non-zero exit stops the arm without spending a counted drive (per test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios; §3 `run` → CI stage selectors, `--live real-model`; §2 Agent-runnable invariants → Deterministic).

## Patterns to follow
- Add a synthetic arm for each new capture behaviour, with the host-path-mask arm as the model (per test-plan §6 Real-model interpretation leg). The workspace-key mask gets its own synthetic arm, covering the key both RAW and in a scrubber-rendered form, in the default suite with no live Pulse.
- Grade a static gate over a committed artifact against its production source, and prove in-suite that it CAN FAIL: a negative arm mutates the real committed input and asserts the error names the offender (per test-plan §6 Coverage-matrix completeness gate, the negative arm and the scenario-assertion audit leg). The same pattern applies to a digest pin: a tampered or elided byte must turn it red.
- Every committed fixture gets one round-trip through the production reader, asserting the properties its consumer depends on (per test-plan §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement, the `lamps_fixture.rs` / `envelope_fixture.rs` / `scenario_audit_gate.rs` set). Evidence under this chunk's `evidence/` (for example an elided copy of the 2026-09-22 capture, if P4 takes that route) follows the same rule.
- Synchronise on an explicit signal, such as an element role, `aria-live`, a JSONL line or an exit code (per test-plan §11 E2E). A frame-readiness race gets fixed by waiting on a readiness signal. Wall-clock waits are admitted only BETWEEN legs, as part of a documented SUT-side firing form, never inside a test.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER mask the `Page/Frame is not ready` failure with a retry (nextest `retries`, a retry-once policy of any kind) or a `sleep(N)` for synchronisation. Never add `continue-on-error` to the a11y gate step. Each of these hides the determinism break rather than removing it (per test-plan §10 Zero-flakiness budget; §11 Quality; §11 E2E; §9 Pipeline structure).
- NEVER run a live-Pulse or real-model leg as a CI gate, and never fake Pulse's reaction as a CI verdict (per test-plan §11 CI; §11 Test Strategy).
- NEVER let the digest route turn a grading assertion into an identity-only check. A file that parses but is semantically inert must still fail at `cargo nextest` (per test-plan §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement).

## Contract bindings
- tests ↔ security: the capture's ingest pipeline and the `capture_paths` guard. The harvest's negative-test duty is `capture_paths_guard` (per test-plan §1 Coverage triggers, fifth class). The rule for what corpus text may sit in test source vs `evidence/` is security's. The harvest is where the test-source side lands.
- tests ↔ a11y: the `a11y` CI job runs the routine arm (`accessibility.e2e.ts`: axe, contrast, violation JSON) through `agent-run.ps1 run --e2e`. The CI red is on that arm, and its fix and witness bind to the a11y harness (per test-plan §9 Pipeline structure, E2E (webview) row; §6 Drivers per surface, desktop-webview row).
- tests ↔ obs: the `--e2e` leg writes one violation record per run at `runs/a11y/<run_id>.jsonl`, checked by the reused `journal_conformance` gate. The `--live real-model` arm's `rule_record` and capture leave the envelope, the `status` read and the JSONL shapes untouched, so test-plan §3 and obs-plan §3 still agree (per test-plan §3 `run` → CI stage selectors; §9 Pipeline structure).
- tests ↔ contracts: the series' design, decision rule and re-fire rule are data in `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md`, and test-plan §9 binds the drive discipline to that document (per test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios).
