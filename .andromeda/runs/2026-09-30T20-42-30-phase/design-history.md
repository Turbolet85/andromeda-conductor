- **2026-06-15-design-token-typography-bundle** — Moved the token block from `@theme` to plain `:root`, with the light override as `@media (prefers-color-scheme: light) { :root { … } }`. The reason is that Tailwind v4 `@theme` tree-shakes non-namespace tokens: only 23 of 34 were emitted, and every `--space-*`, three `--radius-*`, `--motion-micro` and `--ease-quiet` were dropped.
