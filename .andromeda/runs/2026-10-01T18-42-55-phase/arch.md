## Patterns to follow
- The prior series' shape: an add-only posture-contract section fixed before d1, a fixed drive count, captures committed under the chunk's `evidence/`, digest-pinned and graded from the file, a harvest verdict test over the pinned captures — per architecture §Occupied Resources (posture-contract entry: "the drive series (2026-09-29, then 2026-09-30 — each an add-only section fixed before its first drive)").
- The live arm's artifact handling: `run --live real-model` writes `rm.jsonl`, `rm-capture.txt` (rule record, then the capture) and `rm-capture.err` under `$RUNS_DIR/live-suite`, clearing exactly those three non-recursively before its leg, leg-stemmed and git-ignored — so each drive's capture must be copied into `evidence/` before the next drive overwrites it — per architecture §Occupied Resources (`runs/live-suite/{leg}.jsonl` entry) and §Infrastructure Patterns (directory tree).
- The arm strips `RUST_LOG` for the capture's two test invocations (`rule_record` and the capture) and the `.ps1` restores it in a `finally` — per architecture §Occupied Resources (`RUST_LOG` entry).
- Workspace-key agreement is published SUT-side to `{data_dir}/run/workspace-key` and read by the sidecar, falling back to `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_DATA_DIR`; the stamped key was measured in the extended-length `\\?\…` form — the reference point for the CARRY's `app.boot.workspace_key` confirmation. Whether the fix HEAD keeps this publication mechanism is research's question — per architecture §Standard Contracts (Readiness gate, divergence-mechanism paragraph).
- Interpretation attaches at incident CREATION time (measured at Pulse HEAD `83d4060`), which is why the leg needs no multi-digest emission profile; whether the fix HEAD's L4 schema change (decision after analysis) keeps the read-back report's ranked-hypothesis shape that the harvest reads is research's question — per architecture §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture].

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Grading chosen after the drive: any drive fired before its series section (count, rule, re-fire rule) is committed lets the grading fit what happened — per architecture §Occupied Resources (posture-contract entry).
- Widening the type surface to carry the series verdict (a new `Verdict`/`ReportState` variant, an envelope key, a `classify()` change) or promoting the grading out of test tier — per architecture §Established Decisions [Probabilistic-Assertion Policy].
- Conductor code taking on Pulse process management (building or launching Pulse from Conductor's shipped code) — a stated non-goal per architecture §Cross-cutting Patterns (Scope law); launching/building Pulse stays an operator-slotted agent act outside the shipped binaries.
