## Relevance
relevant. The chunk changes emission identity in `conductor-emit` (shape a) or real-model harness timing (shape b), and both touch the determinism invariant, a seam boundary and the real-model leg's posture contract.

## Constraints
- The determinism invariant binds. Per arch §Design Philosophy ("Determinism under a seed") and §Cross-cutting Patterns → Determinism discipline, the same scenario + seed must produce the same emission stream shape. The plan names what the seed shapes as TIMING: bounded per-gap jitter and a cumulative `elapsed_ms` (per arch §Established Decisions [Determinism RNG]). The plan does not say whether `trace_id`/`span_id` belong to "stream shape". Whether shape (a) moves a committed invariant is therefore a question for P3/P4, to answer from the tests that pin it. This extract does not settle it.
- The seeded PRNG is locked. Per arch §Stack and Technologies (Determinism RNG row) and §Established Decisions [Determinism RNG], seeded non-determinism comes from `ChaCha8Rng` via `seed_from_u64`, and `StdRng` was rejected because it has no cross-version stability. Any identity derivation stays on that RNG. Per §Cross-cutting Patterns → Determinism discipline, a per-run input comes from `std::time` or from run-scoped data, never from tokio's virtual clock.
- The seam boundary binds. Per arch §Occupied Resources (Crate names) and §Infrastructure Patterns (directory tree), `conductor-emit` owns exception events and the fingerprint primitive, and it is the one member that does NOT depend on `conductor-core`. Per §Established Decisions [Module Boundaries], a forbidden cross-seam edge must not compile. So a per-run salt must enter `conductor-emit` as a primitive parameter, never as a core type such as a run identity struct. Shape (b) would sit in the run composition root (`conductor-run`) or the harness shells, not in `conductor-emit`.
- The plan already states the identity-collision mechanism. Per arch §Occupied Resources (`contracts/pulse-run-contract.toml` entry), every span `ok_span` builds carries a seeded per-call identity with disjoint warm-up/storm seed ranges. The stated reason is that Pulse's `spans` table is `PRIMARY KEY (trace_id, span_id)` (`andromeda-pulse crates/buffer/src/schema.rs:38`) and `run_consumer` logs-and-skips a colliding row. Three points are for research, not settled here:
  - whether `exception_trace_request` follows the same discipline;
  - whether per-call disjointness extends ACROSS two same-seed runs;
  - whether "logs-and-skips" is the mechanism behind the measured `append_failed` refusal. Check that against Pulse's source.
- The fingerprint stays separate from span identity. Per arch §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] ("Conductor's fingerprint now IS Pulse's derivation"), the fingerprint is blake3 over `exception_type` + `\0` + the normalized stacktrace, and its preimage carries no trace/span id. A span-identity change must leave the fingerprint byte-identical, because the canary and the real-model capture attribute by the cue fingerprint AND freshness (same §, "Freshness is the canary's carrier BY CHOICE").
- The real-model leg is driven only as a pre-stated series. Per arch §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] ("That leg now SHIPS…") and §Occupied Resources (`contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md` entry), the leg runs only under `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md`. That contract fixes the emission profile, the per-leg quiet window and the drive series in add-only sections, before the first drive. It mints no env handle and registers no `agent-run` verb, flag or arm. A shape (b) minimum gap is a quiet-window/timing term, so where it is stated (contract vs code) needs research. Per §Design Philosophy ("Headless-drivable core"), real-model scenarios run headless only.
- The configuration surface is closed. Per arch §Cross-cutting Patterns → Config management and §Occupied Resources (Environment variables), Conductor's handles live under `CONDUCTOR_*`, and `CONDUCTOR_SEED` already exists. Per §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture], `--live` is a selector, not a sixth command. The chunk needs no new handle, verb or arm.

## Patterns to follow
- Seeded per-call identity drawn from disjoint seed ranges (`ok_span`), per arch §Occupied Resources (`contracts/pulse-run-contract.toml` entry). This is the in-tree precedent for shape (a). Whether it gives per-RUN uniqueness or only per-CALL uniqueness is research's question.
- The canary attributes per run through a storm that is "unique per run", per arch §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] ("Freshness is the canary's carrier BY CHOICE"). The scope's observation that d2's canary spans landed points here. How the canary's identity varies is P3's to confirm.
- Raw opentelemetry-proto message structs give byte-level control over identity, per arch §Established Decisions [OTLP Emission Strategy]. W3C Trace Context propagates across emulated service edges, per arch §Conventions (Interface surfaces → Outbound emission). A per-run identity must keep the parent/child linkage consistent within one drive.
- The verdict/error wall applies. Per arch §Design Philosophy ("Outcomes are values, errors are harness faults") and §Cross-cutting Patterns → Verdict/error wall, a SUT-side refusal surfaces as a typed outcome, never as `Result::Err` or a panic.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Do not use `StdRng`, OS entropy or tokio's virtual clock as a source of span identity, per arch §Established Decisions [Determinism RNG] and §Cross-cutting Patterns → Determinism discipline.
- Do not add a `conductor-emit` → `conductor-core` dependency edge to carry a run identity, per arch §Occupied Resources (Crate names: `conductor-emit` EXCEPTED) and §Established Decisions [Module Boundaries].
- Do not change the exception fingerprint's preimage or encoding while changing span identity, per arch §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] (fingerprint derivation paragraph).
