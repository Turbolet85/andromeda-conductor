## Constraints
- The `trace_id` / `span_id` this chunk changes are the PRODUCT fault stream's identity, never self-observation: obs-plan §3 Correlation (no distributed tracing) requires that self-obs carries NO W3C `trace_id`/`traceparent` and correlates by `run_id` alone, and that the only OTLP Conductor speaks is the product stream to `:4317`. Read the §11 Spans/Traces and Logs bans on `trace_id`/`span_id` as bans on SELF-OBS correlation; they neither forbid nor govern per-run identity on the product spans (per obs-plan §3 Correlation; §11 Spans / Traces).
- The real-model posture adds no critical path, span name or span attribute: per obs-plan §4 Scenario "Headless deterministic scenario run…" → Real-model posture, `real-model-interpretation` rides the Path-1 chain with the standard eleven-field envelope, and its interpretation is graded at the harvest tier (`real_model_harvest.rs`), never through the envelope. A fix that lands in this chunk must keep that true (per obs-plan §4 Scenario 1, Real-model posture).
- The `emit.batch` boundary log must keep its wire-shape witness: obs-plan §6 Boundary-call wrappers requires the observed span count, the count of spans a receiver would skip for a MISSING `trace_id`/`span_id`, and the distinct event/attribute KEY NAMES, never values. It is emitted at `debug` on the allowlisted `message` field. A per-run identity must stay populated (non-empty) on every span. Whether today's witness would show anything for a same-identity resend (as opposed to a missing one) is research's question (per obs-plan §6 Boundary-call wrappers; §6 Per-module log levels, `conductor-emit` row).
- Any span attribute or log field the change adds must be a name in `conductor-core::redact::ALLOWLISTED_FIELDS`, because the processor stage drops anything else and it then emits nothing. A per-run value worth recording (such as which identity basis a drive used, or the enforced gap) rides the already-allowlisted `message` field, as the key-set witnesses do (per obs-plan §4 Known-residual path, Required span attributes; §6 Boundary-call wrappers).
- Timing has two clocks: scheduling may use `tokio::time` under `current_thread`, but any measured stamp or elapsed-gap reading (shape (b)'s "measured minimum gap") must come from `std::time::SystemTime`/`Instant`, never tokio's virtual clock (per obs-plan §1 Instrumentation scope, `conductor-timeline` row; §11 Project-specific bans; §5 Per-surface / per-path metrics).
- Determinism under `current_thread`: any per-run identity source must not introduce background tasks or work-stealing that would perturb emission ordering. The same scenario+seed must still yield the same emission-stream SHAPE. Whether span identity counts as "shape" is P3/P4's to settle, not obs's (per obs-plan §1 Instrumentation scope, tokio `current_thread` row; §3 OTel SDK init).
- Every self-obs line the change raises carries the §3 base set (`timestamp_ms`, `level`, `target`, service identity, `run_id`), with no absolute host path in any field (per obs-plan §3 Log format JSON schema, "Two record shapes"; §9 Log conformance check).

## Patterns to follow
- The emitting-side wire-shape witness in obs-plan §6 Boundary-call wrappers (`emit.batch`) is the in-plan pattern for making outbound-request properties observable: counts and key names at `debug` on `message`, ordered deterministically, with no values.
- obs-plan §6 Boundary-call wrappers puts message-borne observables on the allowlisted `message` field rather than minting attributes (the MCP key-set witness, the `mark_incident_resolved` incident id, the coverage roll-up counts). That is the shape to use for any new per-drive note.
- Harvest-tier grading over Pulse's OWN tracing lines is the standing route for SUT-side evidence; obs-plan §4 Scenario 1 Real-model posture and the Restart-suppression and Severity-lifecycle scenarios use it. The d2 refusal signals (`duckdb.append` `reject_reason`, `buffer.tick` `append_rejections`) are Pulse lines of that kind, read at the harvest tier and never through the envelope.
- The canary already reports itself through an existing line: per obs-plan §4 Scenario 1 Real-model posture, the three-storm real-model canary "adds no span or field (each storm logs only the existing message-borne `canary fingerprint computed` line)". That is the precedent for keeping a harness-side change free of telemetry.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER put a product `trace_id`/`span_id` (or any per-run identity value) into a span NAME or as an unbounded attribute. The span-name set is bounded (`scenario.run`, `timeline.execute*`, `emit.batch`, `emit.logs_batch`, `verify.readback*`, `report.generate`, `db.insert_run`, `fault.*`, `tauri.command.*`), and per-trace-ID names are banned (per obs-plan §11 Spans / Traces; §4 Span naming convention).
- NEVER reach for an OTel SDK, `tracing-opentelemetry` or a `traceparent` to generate or carry per-run identity. The product spans are built from `opentelemetry-proto` raw types, and initializing the dormant SDK for self-obs breaks `current_thread` determinism (per obs-plan §3 OTel SDK init; §11 Telemetry Strategy, Universal).
- NEVER log at `info` inside the per-emission path to report identity or gap detail. Use `debug`/`trace` gated by `RUST_LOG=info,conductor_emit=debug`, the global level paired with the target directive (per obs-plan §11 Logs; §6 Per-module log levels).

## Contract bindings
- obs ↔ tests: the "two same-seed drives inside one buffer window both land" test, plus any check on the `emit.batch` wire-shape witness, consumes the §3 self-obs line format. Test-plan §3 owns that format, and obs-plan §3 "Two record shapes" defers to it.
- obs ↔ arch (determinism invariant): whether per-run span identity moves the "same scenario+seed ⇒ same stream shape" contract belongs to architecture. Obs requires only the `current_thread` / no-background-task posture (obs-plan §1, §3 OTel SDK init).
- obs ↔ security: any captured Pulse lines the chunk commits as evidence (the refusal lines) are SUT output under the redaction/scrub rules (obs-plan §11 PII Scrubbing; security-plan owns the capture scrub chain).
