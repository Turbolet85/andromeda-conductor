## Relevance
partial — the chunk adds no UI surface, component, key binding, rendered state or aria-live content: it refactors Rust test targets, adds a `.git`-less skip to `secret_scan_gate` and adds a `stub-server` `cargo check --tests` gate. The only a11y contact is that gate's likely home: `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and/or `scripts/agent-run.{sh,ps1}`, which also carry the `a11y` job and the `run --e2e` routine-arm path.

## Constraints
- The `a11y` CI job's ROUTINE arm (job `a11y`, `windows-2022`, invoked through `scripts/a11y-token-witness.ps1` → `agent-run.ps1 run --e2e`, plus the reused `journal_conformance` gate and the violation-record upload) is a standing CI gate. Any `ci.yml` edit for the new `stub-server` gate must leave that job's steps, runner, driver-pin and invocation unchanged (per a11y-plan §9 CI Integration, Pipeline integration; §3 CI integration).
- `CONDUCTOR_A11Y_STRICT` and the harness shells' printed-verdict assertions (the `Spec Files:` failed count, the per-spec skip tally against the expected-skip SET, the driven-session banner) are what keep a skipped-green a11y run from reading as a pass. An edit to `scripts/agent-run.{sh,ps1}` or `ci.yml` must not remove, rename or bypass them (per a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns → CI, "NEVER skip a11y on CI/operator-gate failure").
- The a11y step is fail-fast. No `continue-on-error`, mocha/nextest `retries`, or retry-once policy may reach the `a11y` job as a side effect of restructuring the workflow (per a11y-plan §10 SLO Invariants; §11 A11y Anti-Patterns → SLO and → CI).
- If the gate lands in `ci.yml`, it belongs in the `rust` job's lane and not the `a11y` job: the a11y job's performance budget (axe-core < 30 s, a fast and unattended routine arm) assumes it carries webview a11y work only (per a11y-plan §10 Performance budget per a11y CI run). Which job hosts the gate is P4's decision; this constraint only rules out the a11y job.

## Patterns to follow
- Make a skip distinguishable from a pass. The a11y harness turns its exit-0 host-tool skip into a hard failure under CI's strict declaration and checks the skip tally against a fixed expected set (per a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns → CI). By analogy, `secret_scan_gate`'s new `.git`-less skip should say that it skipped rather than read as a green pass, and should fire only where no repository exists. The binding rule is the tests/security domain's, so this is a pattern to borrow, not an a11y mandate.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Do not let a workflow or harness-shell edit weaken the a11y gate: no `continue-on-error`, no retries, and no removal of `CONDUCTOR_A11Y_STRICT` or the printed-verdict assertions (per a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns → CI; → SLO).
