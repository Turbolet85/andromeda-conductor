- Amend a §10/§9 obs line from a chunk report that quotes the measured outcome, and add a dated sidecar entry in `obs-plan-amendments.md` (Section / Change / Why / Ref). This is the pattern of the 2026-10-04 §9 hygiene-row entry (per obs-plan §12 Obs Decisions Log → obs-plan-amendments.md).
- Cite a moved test by its new path in the same paragraph that keeps the dated grade, as §4 does with its `03ec944` and `cdb6c1e` re-grades side by side. Never re-date or re-number a grade because its holding test moved (per obs-plan §4 Delegated-timing family).

Never add an allowlisted field to make a mutant visible, because `conductor-core::redact::ALLOWLISTED_FIELDS` drops any non-allowlisted field at the processor stage (per obs-plan §4 Known-residual classification path → Required span attributes; §11 PII Scrubbing).
