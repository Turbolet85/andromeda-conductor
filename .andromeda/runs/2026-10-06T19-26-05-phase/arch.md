## Patterns to follow

- **Add-only dated section with a HEAD re-pin** — the three prior series sections and their re-pins are the shape (architecture §Occupied Resources — On-disk artifacts, the posture-doc entry); moved coordinates in older sections stay as dated records and the new section re-pins what it relies on.
- **Digest-pinned captures graded from their files** — committed capture evidence is held by sha256 and graded from the file, with no capture text in test source (architecture §Stack and Technologies — Hashing / digest; §Standard Contracts — Readiness gate, the corpus-access clause).
- **Per-execution span identity on the production path** — architecture §Cross-cutting Patterns — Determinism discipline requires `execute_scenario` to re-key span identity under a per-execution salt so two same-seed drives never replay an identity a SUT span store still holds. The scope's carried causal claim (d2 of the third series) rests on this; whether the code at HEAD does it is research's question.
- **Attribution by cue fingerprint AND freshness** — architecture §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] names both as the real-model capture's attribution, and records that incident interpretation attaches at creation time, so no multi-digest emission profile is needed to have a hypothesis to grade.
- **Booted posture read from Pulse's own log by TARGET** — where the launch posture carries the bootstrap-window handle, architecture §Occupied Resources — Environment variables (`ANDROMEDA_PULSE_BASELINE_BOOTSTRAP_SECONDS`) requires the reading to come from the named log target, never from the emitting function name.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- **Widening the harness or CI surface for the leg** — no sixth `agent-run` command, no new verb / flag / arm, no new `CONDUCTOR_*` handle, and no CI gate for a leg requiring a live Pulse (architecture §Stack and Technologies — Operator instruments; §Established Decisions [CI/CD]; §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture]).
- **Conductor code managing Pulse or touching its corpus file** — launching `pulse-app` and building its binaries stay operator-leg acts outside any Conductor crate (architecture §Cross-cutting Patterns — Scope law, the no-Pulse-process-management non-goal), and corpus content is reached through the MCP tool surface only, entering the repo solely as scrubbed capture evidence in the chunk's `evidence/` tree (architecture §Standard Contracts — Readiness gate, the corpus-access clause).
- **Reading a green row as the interpretation claim** — architecture §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] states backing is not verifying and a Conductor-green leg does not establish the SUT's interpretation; a graded miss is recorded, never replaced.
