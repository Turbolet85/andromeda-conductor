## Patterns to follow
- **Digest pins ride the existing test-only hashing edge.** architecture.md §Stack and Technologies (the Hashing / digest row) and §Infrastructure Patterns → Build system name `sha2` as a TEST-only `conductor-run` dev-dependency for the real-model harvest's sha256 pins over committed capture evidence, already locked transitively; blake3 stays the only shipped hashing dependency. New capture pins and the section-digest recompute use that edge — no new package, no shipped-crate dependency.
- **The arm's capture files are per-invocation, so each drive is copied out before the next.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources → On-disk artifacts (the `runs/live-suite/{leg}.jsonl` entry) states the real-model arm writes `rm.jsonl`, `rm-capture.txt` and `rm-capture.err` under the runs dir's `live-suite/`, git-ignored, cleared NON-recursively before its leg, the path moving with `CONDUCTOR_RUNS_DIR`. Three drives in one sitting therefore overwrite one another there; whether the harness or a plan step freezes each drive's capture into the chunk's `evidence/` is research's question, and the plan must name it between drives.
- **The grading rule's target is one of two registered service identities.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources → Service / process names (the emitted OTLP service identities entry) registers `conductor` (the scenario dispatcher) and `conductor-canary` (the preflight canary) as deliberately split `service.name` values. The rule's whole-word `conductor` and the fourth series' d3 miss on the hyphenated canary identity are that split; the restated limit for prompt `v2.6` should name the two identities as registered, not re-derive them.
- **A fresh data dir is a workspace-key and scrubber input, not only a path.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources → Environment variables (the `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_DATA_DIR` entry) requires the dir to be propagated to the spawned sidecar and states the read-back filter key is the one `pulse-app` publishes under the data dir, falling back to the dir itself. architecture.md §Established Decisions → [Read-Back Dependency Posture] records the precedent that a data-dir NAME reached the model input redacted by Pulse's scrubber (the 2026-09-29 series). The new leaf's naming terms follow from both.
- **Same-seed drives in one sitting are safe by construction.** architecture.md §Cross-cutting Patterns → Determinism discipline requires span identity to be re-keyed under a per-execution salt on the production path, so repeated same-seed executions never replay a span identity a SUT span store still holds. ## Anti-patterns to avoid
- **Drive first, grade afterwards — or rewrite a dated section.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources → On-disk artifacts (the posture-contract entry) gives the reason every series term is fixed before the first drive: a leg graded after the fact lets the grading be chosen to fit what happened. Editing a prior series' section, or recording the fifth section's digest after d1, breaks the add-only / fixed-before regime.
- **Reading a green row, or a pass, as more than it measured.** architecture.md §Established Decisions → [Read-Back Dependency Posture] states that "Conductor green" does not mean Pulse's interpretation is trustworthy and that backing a capability is not verifying it; and §Established Decisions → [Probabilistic-Assertion Policy] bans hard-failing a model-interpretive miss. A `NotIdentified` drive is never coerced to `Fail` at the row, never replaced, and an `Identified` series is stated as its three drives.
- **Widening the surface to serve the series.** A new `CONDUCTOR_*` handle, a sixth `agent-run` command, a recursive delete in a harness shell, or a direct read of Pulse's corpus file are each excluded — per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → Environment variables (the reserved namespace covers only artifacts the document already defines), §Occupied Resources → On-disk artifacts (the `runs/live-suite/{leg}.jsonl` entry: the harness ships no recursive delete) and §Standard Contracts → Readiness gate (the corpus-access paragraph: `corpus.db` is SUT-owned, reached through the MCP tool surface only).
