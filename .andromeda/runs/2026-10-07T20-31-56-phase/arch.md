## Patterns to follow
- Workspace placement: series pins and verdict/ref tests are test-tier code in `conductor-run` (architecture.md §Occupied Resources — Crate names; §Infrastructure Patterns → Directory structure (crate-per-seam Cargo workspace)). No new workspace member and no new dependency edge.
- Digest pins over committed captures use `sha2`, registered as a TEST-only `conductor-run` dev-dependency that adds no package (architecture.md §Stack and Technologies — Hashing / digest). Whether `conductor-run`'s manifest already declares it is research's question.
- The arm's working files live under `runs/live-suite/` (`rm.jsonl`, `rm-capture.txt`, `rm-capture.err`), git-ignored and cleared non-recursively before the leg (architecture.md §Occupied Resources — On-disk artifacts, the `runs/live-suite/{leg}.jsonl` entry). Each drive's capture is copied into the chunk's `evidence/` before the next drive clears them.
- `real-model-interpretation` is declare-only from birth: it grades nothing through the envelope, its interpretation claim is graded at the harvest tier, and its success row lands `ManualCheck` (architecture.md §Standard Contracts — Run report envelope). The series verdict is read from the harvest, never from the envelope's `verdict`.
- The service identities `conductor` (scenario dispatcher) and `conductor-canary` (preflight canary) are split deliberately (architecture.md §Occupied Resources — Service / process names, the emitted OTLP service identities entry). The grading rule's whole-word `conductor` rests on that split; the series changes neither name.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Putting Pulse process management into a shipped binary or a harness shell. architecture.md §Cross-cutting Patterns (Scope law) lists "no Pulse process management" as a stated non-goal; the Pulse build and the `pulse-app` launch/stop stay session acts under the overseer's grant, outside `scripts/agent-run.{sh,ps1}` and every crate.
- Persisting corpus content outside the one ratified, scoped exception. architecture.md §Standard Contracts (Readiness gate — the corpus-access passage) requires captures to enter only a chunk's `evidence/` tree, sha256 digest-pinned and graded from the file, with no capture text in test source.
- Running the real-model scenario through `conductor suite` or the GUI and reading the result as a drive. architecture.md §Design Philosophy (Headless-drivable core, thin shells) requires the real-model posture to run headless through `conductor run` alone; the other two keep the deterministic gate and record `Blocked`.
