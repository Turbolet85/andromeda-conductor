## Relevance
relevant — the chunk is a real-model series: an operator-gated live leg (three drives through `agent-run run --live real-model`) plus default-suite harvest tests over its digest-pinned captures, both of which test-plan specifies.

## Constraints
- The leg has one entry and one footing: test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm (Real-model interpretation leg) requires `scenarios/real-model-interpretation.toml` to run only through `agent-run run --live real-model`, operator-gated, driven only as a pre-stated series of `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md`, never in CI and with no §9 stage-table row. The sixth series must be such a pre-stated series section, not a new entry path (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm — Real-model interpretation leg).
- Series discipline is a test-plan mandate, not only a scope choice: test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios requires the series be fixed before its first drive, every drive recorded and never replaced, a re-fire allowed once and only for a Conductor-side environment or pipeline fault, and the pickup recorded as a measurement and never budgeted (per test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios).
- The interpretation claim grades at the HARVEST tier, in the default suite: test-plan §6 requires the grade to be computed in `crates/conductor-run/tests/real_model_harvest.rs` over the drive's scrubbed capture, against a rule whose span is recorded into the capture AHEAD of the leg and asserted to predate it, with the three-way outcome mapping (`Identified` / `NotIdentified` / nothing-to-grade) stated there. Whether the harvest's existing rule-predates assertion already covers a new series' captures without a per-series arm is research's question (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm — Real-model interpretation leg).
- The grading rule may not move: test-plan §6 requires the harvest to hold the recorded 2026-09-23 rule as a byte-exact prefix of the current rule. Scope item 9 leaves the rule unchanged, so this chunk must leave that assertion and the rule's recorded bytes untouched (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm — Real-model interpretation leg).
- Every committed capture is evidence under a digest pin: test-plan §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement requires the harvest to read captures read-only from the chunk's `evidence/` tree, `CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR`-anchored, each checked against its sha256 pin before any grade reads it, and never copied elsewhere as a fixture (per test-plan §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement).
- The capture population is pinned by count: test-plan §6 requires a population arm holding every committed `rm-capture*.txt` at zero un-elided keyed fingerprint values and at a fixed point of the elision, beside its inverse control, and a source arm holding that no capture text sits in test source. Three new committed captures move that pinned population; whether the pin is a literal count this chunk must edit, and whether the new files' names fall inside the arm's glob, is research's question (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm — Real-model interpretation leg).
- The default suite must stay green under both runners with no retry: test-plan §4 Framework requires `cargo test -p <crate>` (full, non-doc) green beside nextest, and test-plan §10 Zero-flakiness budget forbids nextest `retries` > 0 and treats a runner-dependent result as a determinism break. The new harvest tests read only committed files, so they must hold no process-global or live dependency (per test-plan §4 Framework; per test-plan §10 Zero-flakiness budget).

## Patterns to follow
- One verdict test per series, named by the series: test-plan §6 records each prior series by its date, its Pulse sha and its verdict test (`v3_09_is_not_met_by_the_{date}_series`), with "no ref test, no fourth drive" on a not-met series, and holds the capture run's tests apart as importing no verdict arm. Both `2026_10_07` names are taken there (the fifth series and the capture run), so the sixth series' module and test names must collide with neither. The plan describes no MET series and so no ref-test shape — that shape comes from scope item 7 and the matrix acceptance, and whether any ref-test precedent exists in code is research's question (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm — Real-model interpretation leg).
- The `--live real-model` arm's own step order is the firing form to drive, not to re-implement: preconditions `--for` the scenario, the capture binary's pre-build, the non-recursive removal of the three named `runs/live-suite/` files, the `rule_record` into `rm-capture.txt` before the leg (a non-zero exit there stops the arm so no counted drive is spent), the leg, then the capture. Each drive's capture must be taken out of `runs/live-suite/` before the next drive, since that removal clears it (per test-plan §3 `run`).
- `skip_reason` is recorded, never graded: test-plan §6 requires Pulse's `interpretation.incident.skipped` reason to ride each `canary:` line as a trailing field while the rule reads the first token only — the shape scope item 6's ledger rows rely on (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm — Real-model interpretation leg).
- Quiet windows between drives are firing form: test-plan §11 E2E classes a harness-level wait between legs or drives that reproduces a documented SUT-side precondition as part of the firing form, distinct from a sleep used to synchronise inside a test (per test-plan §11 E2E).
- Fast iteration and the full gate: `cargo nextest run -p conductor-run` or `-E 'test(<name>)'` for the harvest tests, then the bundled `scripts/agent-run.sh run` default, which stops at its first non-zero cargo line and includes the `-p conductor-run --features live-pulse` lint line that compiles and lints the capture target without running it (per test-plan §3 `run`).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER run the live-Pulse leg as a CI gate, and never read CI as evidence for the series: CI compiles and lints the `live-pulse` targets and runs none of them (per test-plan §11 CI; per test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios).
- NEVER fake Pulse's reaction as a verdict: the synthetic arms prove the grading function's outcome arms, and only a committed live capture carries the series verdict (per test-plan §11 Test Strategy).
- NEVER read the leg's process exit as the grade, and never add a retry to reach one: `Blocked` / `ManualCheck` / `CalibrationRegion` are reported envelope states at exit 0, so a `NotIdentified` drive does not fail the leg's exit — the verdict is the harvest's, and a retry-once policy is banned (per test-plan §11 E2E; per test-plan §11 Quality).

## Contract bindings
- tests ↔ obs: test-plan §3 `run` requires the `--live real-model` selector to leave the run-report envelope, the `status` disk read and the JSONL shapes untouched, so test-plan §3 and obs-plan §3 keep agreeing. The chunk adds no verb and no selector, so no obs shape should move; a real-model envelope reads `verdict` null with a non-`Pass` state on the nothing-to-grade arm (test-plan §6), which is the envelope's existing null arm.
- tests ↔ security: the scrub chain a capture must pass before entering `evidence/` is security-plan's scoped exception (§Input Validation); test-plan §6 holds its test-side half — the digest pins, the population arm and the no-capture-text source arm. The supply-chain gate's form is test-plan §9 Supply-chain audit form (bare `cargo audit` beside `cargo deny check`), with tool floors owned by security-plan §Dependency Security. The digest pins use the test-only `sha2` dev-dependency test-plan §6 names, so no dependency delta is expected; whether one arises is research's question.
- tests ↔ arch: test-plan §3 `run` requires the selector to add no port, env var or `CONDUCTOR_*` handle (arch §Occupied Resources); a fresh data-dir leaf and a new posture-contract section register nothing there by this plan's terms. Scope item 10's byte threshold is arch's, not this plan's.
- tests ↔ a11y: none — test-plan §9 Live-Pulse scenarios requires the `real-model` selector never to drive `a11y:driven`.
- Plan-side record (wrap's act, masters read-only in phase and implement): test-plan §6's leg paragraph carries one dated record per series and states that the series set is the posture contract's dated sections, never a count restated there. The sixth series' outcome is a candidate amendment to that paragraph at the wrap; test-plan §2 Agent-runnable invariants and §9 Live-Pulse scenarios name the series generically and need none.
