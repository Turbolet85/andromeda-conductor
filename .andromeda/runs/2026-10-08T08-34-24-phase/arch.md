## Patterns to follow
- **Outcomes as typed values, never panics, on SUT-authored input.** architecture.md §Design Philosophy (Outcomes are values, errors are harness faults) and §Cross-cutting Patterns (Verdict/error wall): a pairing that meets a tick with no following prompt, parse or incident line returns a classified value. Whether the test-tier classifier already holds this for a segment that ends at end-of-log is research's question.
- **Pulse's write-path order is the recorded basis for an order-based pairing.** architecture.md §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] records the path as OTLP → L1 → L2 RetryStorm cue → L3 digest → L4 inference → incident, and records that a new incident is deduped against an OPEN incident of the same `(kind, scope, scope_id)` tuple. Those coordinates are transcribed at Pulse HEAD `83d4060`; whether they hold at the HEAD the sixth series pinned, and whether they fix the order of two same-millisecond lines, is research's question.
- **Attribution by cue fingerprint AND freshness.** architecture.md §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture] requires the real-model capture to attribute by both; telling the scenario's own storm digest apart from a canary digest should not weaken either carrier. Whether Pulse's log lines carry a usable identity for that is research's question (the scope states they carry no digest identity).
- **Capture artifacts keep their registered names and clearing idiom.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources (On-disk artifacts — `runs/live-suite/{leg}.jsonl`) registers the real-model arm's `rm.jsonl`, `rm-capture.txt` and `rm-capture.err`, cleared non-recursively before the leg; the fix changes what a future capture prints, not where or under which name.
- **Directory roles hold.** architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → Directory structure (crate-per-seam Cargo workspace) places the posture file among the `contracts/` members and counts it as one of the two no Rust code reads.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- **Capture text in test source.** architecture.md §Standard Contracts (Readiness gate — Corpus access) records the remedied state as every committed capture sha256 digest-pinned and graded from its file, with no capture text in test source; a fixture for the d1/d2/d3 shapes built by pasting committed capture or Pulse-log lines into a `.rs` file re-opens that breach. A fixture of stamps and line kinds is the shape to test; where its line is drawn is security's ruling.
- **A new surface for a test-tier fix.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources (Environment variables) reserves the `CONDUCTOR_*` namespace for registered handles, and architecture.md §Stack and Technologies (Operator instruments) holds the harness at five commands; no new handle, `--live` selector, verb or on-disk artifact to host the new tests.
- **Reading the posture contract from Rust.** architecture.md §Occupied Resources (On-disk artifacts — `contracts/pulse-real-model-leg-posture.md`) keeps it reader-less; a test that parses the contract to derive the classification would move it into the runtime-read regime the four manifests carry.
