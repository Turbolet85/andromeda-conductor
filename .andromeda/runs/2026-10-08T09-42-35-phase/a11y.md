- Proof stated with its configuration, not as a bare verdict: the form a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns § Strategy uses for the routine arm's CI proof is the form the close record uses for any a11y-backed basis.
- Owner plus "what gates in its place" for a claim CI cannot assert: a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns § Strategy records each operator-local claim with its single owner and an explicit statement that the in-place gate is not a substitute. This matches the close record's "verified or deferred on a measured basis with a named owner".
- Dated premise-correction kept beside the superseded reading rather than rewriting it: a11y-plan §9 CI Integration (Platform) shows the form; an open item named at the close is worded as an open reading with its date, not as a silent correction.
- Plan changes travel as recorded amendments, never as an edit inside the phase loop: a11y-plan §12 A11y Decisions Log names `a11y-plan-amendments.md` as the live home, and the plan's header bars edits during phase-loop runs. If the wrap turns the `verdict` item into anything written, it goes there.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER claim WCAG conformance without machine-verifiable evidence — a close record that summarizes an a11y-backed capability as "accessible" or "conformant" beyond its committed evidence breaks a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns § Universal.
- NEVER treat the cli surface as accessibility-assertable — the `[PASS]`/`[FAIL]` prefixes and text-paired output are output-stream discipline and must not appear in the close record as an a11y verification basis, per a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns § Universal.
