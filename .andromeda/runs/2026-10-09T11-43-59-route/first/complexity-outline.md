## Project signals (from arch + input)

- **Product type:** as architecture §Project Intent still words it — "Desktop control-panel app (Tauri 2) over a headless-drivable Rust core", verifying "via an operator checklist for visual claims". That is the 0.3.0 system. What 0.4.0 DELIVERS is the intent's: a console harness that drives an engine on another host (`v4-08`, `v4-09`). The masters change at wraps, never here.
- **Surfaces in current scope:** one surface survives and carries everything — the command line (`conductor-cli` and `scripts/agent-run.sh`). Two engine-facing paths are NEW: emission over a network behind a token (`v4-10`), and reading through the door an investigating agent uses (`v4-12`). One run form is NEW: a world and a schedule (`v4-13`…`v4-18`). RETIRED in this version: Conductor's window (`v4-01`), the grading of Pulse's window (`v4-03`) and of its local model (`v4-02`), the shared machine (`v4-04`), the other operating systems (`v4-07`).
- **Crates / modules:** 9 workspace members today (architecture §Occupied Resources — Crate names). One leaves whole (`conductor-tauri`, `v4-01`); which members the new run form adds or re-shapes is the phase loop's to decide.
- **Capabilities (Established Decisions count):** 22 in architecture §Established Decisions. This version re-opens most of them: the outbound address, the read-back client, the read-back posture, the timing-tolerance model, the probabilistic-assertion policy, the accepted capability set, the real-time strategy, deployment and CI. Its own capability count is 24 (`v4-01`…`v4-24`), nine of them removals (`v4-01`…`v4-08`, and the form's in `v4-16`) and one a closing audit (`v4-24`).
- **Standard Contracts:** 4 in architecture §Standard Contracts (the readiness gate, the liveness check, the run-report envelope with its per-check record, the live update channel). All four change subject or leave: the gate is re-subjected to the door (`v4-12`), the liveness check loses its fixed address (`v4-04`), the envelope loses tiers and the non-machine states (`v4-02`, `v4-03`, `v4-05`) and gains events and reactions (`v4-19`, `v4-20`), the channel leaves with the window (`v4-01`).
- **Cross-cutting concerns:** 6 in architecture §Cross-cutting Patterns (config management, development style, determinism discipline, verdict/error wall, trust boundary, scope law). Determinism and the verdict/error wall carry over unchanged. The trust boundary ("entirely on the loopback dev host … no secrets") is replaced by `v4-10` and `v4-11`; the scope law's "no scenario without a P-ID" is re-based by `v4-06`.
- **Security tier (cross-input):** `Minimal` today (the security rule every agent loads: "local-only loopback tool, no network service, no secrets"). `v4-11` has the security master re-rate Conductor for a program that holds credentials and reaches another host; the new tier is that chunk's to state, not this outline's.
- **Project signals from input.md:** the 0.1.0 creator brief. Still true of it: the scope law, the deterministic seeded timeline, the journal as ground truth, agent-driven development, "not a load-tester". No longer the product: "Runs next to a real Pulse instance on the dev host", "via an operator checklist where the reaction is visual", the control panel, operator pauses, "NO multi-target / distributed generation … local dev host only". `v4-08` owns the restatement.
- **Existing Scopes:** None (architecture §Existing Scopes). The project is greenfield-born, not adopted; 0.3.0 closed complete at `97dea7f`.

## Calibration outcome

- **Hierarchy depth:** 3 levels
- **Reasoning:** One surviving surface against 9 crates would sit in the two-level band, but this version re-opens most of the 22 Established Decisions and all 4 Standard Contracts and carries 24 capabilities in five intent themes, so three epochs hold seven or more distinct concerns and need a sub-block tier to stay legible (the retirements of Epoch 5, the long run of Epoch 7, the close of Epoch 8).

### Adjustments applied (optional)

- **Mid epochs are shaped from this version's requirements, not from architecture's surfaces** (the operator's directive for this run, §PHASE 0). `phase-0/calibration.md:54-61` derives mid epochs from the surfaces architecture §Project Intent names, and those are the 0.3.0 system's: a "Desktop control-panel app (Tauri 2)", a "control panel" seam, a harness that "runs next to a real Pulse instance on the dev host". The precedent is this project's own last next-version outline (`.andromeda/runs/2026-09-11T06-27-07-route/complexity-outline.md`), whose five epochs are each named for their requirement ids.
- **A surface the version retires gets NO surface epoch.** No epoch exists for Conductor's window (`v4-01`), for the grading of Pulse's window (`v4-03`), for the grading of Pulse's local model (`v4-02`), for the shared machine (`v4-04`) or for the other operating systems (`v4-07`). Each retirement is work INSIDE the epochs the requirements shape: `v4-01` and `v4-07` in Foundation, `v4-02`, `v4-03`, `v4-04` and the old form of `v4-16` in Epoch 5, after what replaces them is reachable.
- **Epoch order follows the intent's §6 table** — what the engine offers at each of Pulse's checks, re-read here from `andromeda-pulse-0.4.0/working-route.md` at `b3ac58a` (10 epochs, 77 entries by its commit subject). The signal is concrete: Pulse's `Door inside the engine's process` sits in its Epoch 1 and retires the stdio sidecar in the same entry; its network receiver opens in Epoch 4; its memory-under-a-long-run and replay entries sit in Epoch 5; its bad-version scenario in Epoch 10.
- **No tier bump.** The security tier moves off `Minimal` inside this version (`v4-11`), but no master states `Hardened` and the intent names no regulated domain; the rise in security work is carried as concerns of Epoch 6, not as an extra hierarchy level.

## Epoch outline (8 epochs)

### Epoch 1 — Foundation: a console program on Linux, read through the door

- **Scope:** Conductor becomes a console program built and checked on Linux alone, reads a live console engine through its door, and reaches one recorded engine-backed verdict from the command line (`v4-01`, `v4-07`, `v4-09`, `v4-12`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 5
- **Concerns:**
  - The window leaves first, because every later chunk would otherwise have to keep it compiling: the `conductor-tauri` crate, the `frontend` and `a11y` CI jobs, the window scripts, the run script's e2e and a11y arms (`v4-01`; architecture §Stack — Desktop shell / Desktop frontend, §Established Decisions [Real-time Strategy], [CI/CD]).
  - The panel-shaped types outside that crate, and the proof that every function is reachable from the command line (`v4-01`, `v4-09`).
  - Base CI on a Linux runner with fast feedback; the PowerShell twin and the Windows-only steps leave (`v4-07`; architecture §Established Decisions [CI/CD], [Deployment]).
  - The door as the read path, on the engine's own host — the first engine-facing capability to land, since from Pulse's `Door inside the engine's process` no sidecar exists (`v4-12`; architecture §Established Decisions [MCP Read-Back Client]).
  - The reachability of the new engine-backed gate: one trivial run, command line → engine's receiver → door → recorded verdict; and the engine-less tier saying that it claims Conductor's own behaviour only (`v4-12`; `phase-1/synthesis-protocol.md` §Placement).

### Epoch 2 — One way to describe a run

- **Scope:** A run is a world and a schedule of declared events on one timeline, validated when it is described — built beside the single-list form, which is neither extended nor adapted (`v4-13`, `v4-15`, `v4-16`, `v4-17`, `v4-18`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 5
- **Concerns:**
  - A world of three to five named services with call links and versions; a fault in one felt in another (`v4-13`; architecture §Service / process names — emitted OTLP service identities).
  - One timeline for the whole run with overlapping events, under the seed (`v4-15`; architecture §Design Philosophy — Determinism under a seed, §Cross-cutting — Determinism discipline).
  - Declared truth on every event and on every healthy stretch (`v4-17`).
  - The run description as the one loadable form, bounds-checked at load, every run and event naming an engine capability (`v4-16`, `v4-06`; architecture §Conventions — Config conventions, §Established Decisions [Validation Library], [Scenario Config Format]).
  - Discrimination decided when the run is described: a control without the event, and a refusal of an assertion that passes without its stimulus (`v4-18`).

### Epoch 3 — The reaction on the timeline

- **Scope:** The gate, the attribution, the reaction log and the per-event grading of a run read through the door (`v4-12`, `v4-19`, `v4-20`, part of `v4-05`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 5
- **Concerns:**
  - The gate before a run proves three things through the door, each with its own blocked name (`v4-12`; architecture §Standard Contracts — Readiness gate).
  - A run states the engine state it starts from; what the engine already held is told apart from what the run raised; the door's one write is named (`v4-12`).
  - Per reaction: when the engine spoke, and the structured facts it named; the content boundary restated (`v4-19`; architecture §Standard Contracts — Corpus access).
  - Per event: caught or not, minutes after onset, declared service and cause named; nothing reported in a healthy stretch; machine-only verdicts on one stated time base (`v4-20`, `v4-05`; architecture §Established Decisions [Timing-Tolerance Model], [Probabilistic-Assertion Policy]).
  - The run record, journal, report and index for the one form; the verdict's absent arm stated (`v4-19`, `v4-20`; architecture §Standard Contracts — Run report envelope; residual `residuals.md:19`).

### Epoch 4 — Regression proofs through the door

- **Scope:** Each detection the engine still claims gets its short run and its control, read through the door, on a capability set re-based on Pulse's current record — Conductor's readiness for Pulse's `Theme 0 checked by the external harness` (`v4-06`, `v4-16`, `v4-18`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 5
- **Concerns:**
  - The accepted set, its classification, the coverage record and the pinned references re-derived from Pulse's re-based record — AFTER Pulse's `Capability record re-based` exists, never guessed (`v4-06`; architecture §Established Decisions [Accepted Capability Set]).
  - The fourteen kept detection subjects as short runs, in three families (rates, latency and error scope; identity and capture; quiet and lifecycle) (`v4-16`; intent §3).
  - The three unclear subjects decided by Pulse's record (`v4-16`, `v4-06`).
  - Every run's event-less control graded "nothing reported" (`v4-18`).
  - The whole set driven against a live engine and recorded (`v4-16`; intent §7 point 3).

### Epoch 5 — What leaves with the old form

- **Scope:** Everything that existed only for the shared machine, the local model, Pulse's window, the single-list form and the short-run tiers leaves, each chunk naming what leaves with it — after its replacement is reachable (`v4-02`, `v4-03`, `v4-04`, `v4-05`, `v4-16`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 2
- **Estimated chunks:** 7
- **Concerns:**
  - The log-file harvest that holds today's real proofs, and the operator-started live suite (`v4-04`).
  - The spawned sidecar, its pinned stdio contract, and the gate's shared-directory, workspace-key, environment and `PATH` preconditions (`v4-04`; architecture §Service / process names — launched child process, §Environment variables).
  - The port fault and the five shared-machine scenarios (`v4-04`; architecture §Occupied Resources — Ports, §Cross-cutting — Trust boundary).
  - Local-model grading: the scenarios, the posture contract, the launch term, the tests, the live leg (`v4-02`).
  - Window grading: the scenarios, the operator hold, the checklist, the timing contract and its harvest (`v4-03`).
  - The single-list form itself with its remaining files, its run path, the assertion audit ledger and its gate (`v4-16`).
  - The vocabulary that served only them: the posture, the model's verdict, the degraded and manual states and the lamp, the drive-and-observe class, the tiers, the per-scenario latency, the over-tier list (`v4-02`, `v4-03`, `v4-05`).

### Epoch 6 — The network and the credentials

- **Scope:** The stream and the door are reached on another host with credentials given at run time — Conductor's readiness for Pulse's `Theme 1 checked by the external harness` (`v4-04`, `v4-10`, `v4-11`, part of `v4-20`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 1
- **Estimated chunks:** 4
- **Concerns:**
  - The two-host path reachable: a trivial run from a second host to a recorded verdict. It cannot sit in Foundation — the engine's network receiver opens in Pulse's Epoch 4 (`v4-10`).
  - The channel's refusals, and one path on one machine as on two; the fixed local address gone (`v4-10`, `v4-04`).
  - Credentials: where a run reads them from, where they never appear, the blocked report for an engine Conductor cannot authenticate to, the security rating restated (`v4-11`).
  - The difference between the two hosts' clocks measured, and a verdict it could change reported as such (`v4-20`).

### Epoch 7 — The living world and the long run

- **Scope:** A background that makes silence mean something, the four event families, a run of hours with its measurements and its whole-run grading, and the replay (`v4-05`, `v4-14`, `v4-15`, `v4-20`, `v4-21`, `v4-22`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 2
- **Estimated chunks:** 7
- **Concerns:**
  - The compressed day, ordinary noise, harmless errors at a low rate (`v4-14`).
  - The four families of the first long run; the slow families emitted, "not reported" recorded as the starting point (`v4-15`; intent §6).
  - The load a run may put on the engine re-measured against the engine with its store on disk (`v4-05`; architecture §On-disk artifacts — the load envelope).
  - The record and the journal written as the run goes, bounded in memory; an interrupted run still readable (`v4-21`).
  - The engine's memory against 1 GB and its database file's growth, measured over the run (`v4-21`).
  - Whole-run grading: caught, minutes after onset, reports per healthy hour, named service and cause (`v4-20`).
  - A recorded stream replayed faster than recorded, with the same reactions (`v4-22`).

### Epoch 8 — Polish & ship

- **Scope:** The pair's end-to-end scenario on two hosts, the ten-hour run, the records restated, the no-survivor audit and the close (`v4-06`, `v4-08`, `v4-21`, `v4-23`, `v4-24`).
- **Drilldown depth:** 2
- **Estimated chunks:** 6
- **Concerns:**
  - The bad-version rollout read from the status line and the report (`v4-23`).
  - The large model's answer recorded against declared truth — a reading, never a gate (`v4-23`).
  - One completed run of ten hours or more with its graded timeline (`v4-21`; intent §7 point 2).
  - The first-read records restated (`v4-08`).
  - The no-survivor question asked of the tree (`v4-24`).
  - The close: Conductor's accepted set agreeing with Pulse's capability record; every capability verified on measured evidence (`v4-06`).

## Notes for Phase 1 synthesis

- **Replace, never adapt.** The new run form (Epochs 2–4) is built beside the single-list form and the old run path; no chunk extends the spawned sidecar, the log harvest or a single-list scenario file. Epoch 5 removes them once the short runs through the door exist (intent §8).
- **Inside Epoch 5 the order is: harvest and live suite → sidecar and shared gate → port fault → model → window → the form → the vocabulary.** The vocabulary leaves last because the old run path is its last user.
- **`v4-06` depends on another project's record.** The re-base chunk opens Epoch 4 and says in its own line that it follows Pulse's `Capability record re-based`; the agreement of the two sets is checked again at the close.
- **Gate reachability.** The engine-backed gate's reachability chunk is Foundation's last. The two-host path's reachability cannot be in Foundation (Pulse's receiver opens in its Epoch 4); it opens Epoch 6 — a place where `phase-1/validation.md` [GATE_REACHABILITY] cannot be met as written, to be reported.
- **Retired plans.** `design-system.md`, `layout-templates.md` and `a11y-plan.md` have the window as their subject; they are cited only by the chunks that retire what they describe, and the bootstrap merging map yields no chunk for a retired surface.
- **Where Conductor meets Pulse's checks** (the intent's §6): `Theme 0 checked` ← end of Epoch 4; `Theme 1 checked` and the memory-under-a-long-run entry ← Epoch 6 and the long-run half of Epoch 7; `Theme 2`–`Theme 4 checked` ← Epochs 2–4 and 7 already hold the world's versions, declared truth and reaction log; `Theme 5 checked` and the bad-version scenario ← Epoch 8.
