## Patterns to follow
- Identify CI jobs by NAME, never by `ci.yml` line coordinates, which move as the workflow changes (per a11y-plan §3 A11y testing tool pick, Configuration). The `a11y` and `frontend` jobs are removed and verified by name.
- A printed verdict outranks an unisolated exit code (per a11y-plan §11 CI). Read "the a11y job is gone" from the push's CI run job list by name, not from a green overall status.
- The plan's measurement records cite committed evidence outside the crate (`conductor-0.3.0/chunks/2026-09-30-the-screen-reader-content-findings-fixed/evidence/nvda-pass.json` per a11y-plan §4 ARIA Patterns & Roles; `conductor-0.3.0/chunks/2026-09-30-interpretation-re-proven-on-a-clean-named-data-dir/evidence/race-witness.md` per a11y-plan §9 CI Integration). These are closed-version records, not on the entry's closed removal list. Whether any removal sweep reaches them is a P3/P4 question, never an assumption.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Never retire the a11y gate by disabling it in place: no `if: false`, no `continue-on-error`, no skipped-green arm or expected-skip SET left behind with nothing to skip. A gate that stops failing without being removed is exactly the skipped-green state a11y-plan §11 CI bans. The job, its steps and its strict-verdict plumbing leave whole.
- Never treat the cli surface as accessibility-assertable (per a11y-plan §11 Universal). The window's removal transfers no WCAG claim to `conductor`. Its `[PASS]`/`[FAIL]`/`[HOLD]`/`[BLOCKED]` prefixes stay output-stream discipline, not an a11y verification path.
- Never leave a WCAG conformance claim standing without machine-verifiable evidence (per a11y-plan §11 Strategy). Once nothing produces axe/colorjs.io JSON, any surviving text that asserts conformance is unbacked, so the expected amendments retire it rather than leave it.
