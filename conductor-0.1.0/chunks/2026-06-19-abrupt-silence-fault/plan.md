## Goal
Ship `AbruptSilence` — a fault helper in `conductor-faults` that models an **abrupt, permanent emission stop
with no resume**. This is the P-014 activity-floor "the service died" lever: a fresh service that has been
actively emitting (establishing an activity floor) goes abruptly silent and never resumes, and Pulse raises an
activity-floor cue ~30s after the last emission (input.md:71, :106). It is the **terminal counterpart to
`EmissionGap`** (P-015): where `EmissionGap` owns a finite, exact gap `Duration`, validates the 20s restart
floor, and *resumes* (Pulse reads a restart), abrupt-silence has **no resume boundary** and **no finite length
to own** — permanence is the structural *absence* of a `Duration`. `gap.rs`'s module-doc already
forward-references "the permanent-silence fault (P-014)"; this chunk fulfills that reference.

Per the P4 design decision (user-confirmed): the helper is an **infallible, parameterless marker**. Unlike
its two siblings it acquires no OS resource (`PortOccupier`'s socket) and has no bound to validate
(`EmissionGap`'s 20s floor), so there is **nothing to fail on** — it is the crate's first infallible helper
and leaves `FaultError` untouched (the `#[non_exhaustive]` enum stays ready for future fallible helpers). This
applies the same principle the gap chunk used (a *named* error only for a *real* threshold) to its logical
conclusion: no threshold ⇒ no error. Determinism is satisfied trivially by construction — there is no seed and
no parameter, so same scenario ⇒ same stop (per obs-plan §1; mirrors the `EmissionGap` / fingerprint
seed-independence rationale, testing-rule 2026-06-16/2026-06-18). The chunk ships the standalone *structure*
only: no OTLP emission, no timeline wiring, no MCP read-back, no verdict (those are Epochs 7/8 per arch
§Cross-cutting Patterns).

## Implementation notes
- **Module name `silence.rs`** (not `abrupt_silence.rs`): files in this crate are concept-named with a
  descriptive type (`gap.rs`→`EmissionGap`), and `silence` matches the obs `fault.silence` span name. The
  permanent stop is *the* silence; the gap is a gap.
- **`Default` derive is load-bearing for clippy:** a `new() -> Self` with no args trips
  `clippy::new_without_default` under `-D warnings` unless `Default` is derived — derive it (and it gives the
  free `default() == new()` test).
- **`resumes(&self)` stays on `AbruptSilence` only** — do not retrofit a symmetric method onto `EmissionGap`
  or introduce a shared trait; the contrast is documented + asserted, not abstracted (YAGNI; keeps the change
  additive and scoped).
- **Carried/deferred (do not action here):** the obs `fault.silence` span lists a `fault_duration_ms`
  attribute (obs-plan §4) that has no value for a *permanent* stop; the null/sentinel-duration handling is an
  Epoch-7/8 timeline-wiring concern, explicitly out of this chunk (flagged so it is not lost).
- Keep the module-doc altitude consistent with `gap.rs` — explain the P-ID, the permanence/no-resume
  contract, the determinism-by-construction property, and the infallibility rationale in 1-2 short paragraphs.
