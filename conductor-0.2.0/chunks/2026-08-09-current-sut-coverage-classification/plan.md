## Goal

Conductor's coverage classification stops at `P-060` while the accepted capability set — data, since
`2026-08-08-sut-capability-manifest` — reaches `P-082`. `conductor_core::check_sut_drift` already gates that
difference against a pinned residual ledger (`KNOWN_UNCLASSIFIED`, 22 ids). This chunk closes the gap for good:
**every capability the manifest accepts carries exactly one classification, with zero unclassified entries**, and
the "not Conductor's to verify" boundary becomes a recorded decision rather than an absence.

Three things follow. First, a **fourth `CoverageMode` variant** — `not-conductors` — distinct from `static-only`
(which means *no dynamic telemetry dimension*; the new one means *outside Conductor's remit by standing
non-goal*). It is a classification axis only: `Verdict` and `ReportState` stay closed, no seventh lamp, and no row
renders as `Blocked` or `Fail` for being out of remit (per architecture.md §Standard Contracts, a11y-plan §6
State-naming crosswalk, design-system.md §Anti-Patterns / Rejected Defaults). Second, **the 22 new rows**,
classified per intent §4 F2 + §5 boundary with titles and categories sourced verbatim from Pulse's own
`andromeda-pulse-0.3.0/requirements.md` and `verification-matrix.json`. Third, **`KNOWN_UNCLASSIFIED` empties** —
the anti-rot mechanism working as designed, since the drift check fails on a pinned-but-now-classified id.

Two decisions were taken by the operator at P4. **Representation:** the classification stays **code-native** —
`static COVERAGE` widens in Rust and `contracts/pulse-capabilities.toml` stays ids-only. This preserves the
documented no-runtime-IO property, keeps `coverage_matrix() -> &'static [CapabilityRow]` so no consumer signature
moves, and keeps `check_sut_drift` a genuine two-source comparison instead of a self-check (per architecture.md
§Established Decisions [Accepted Capability Set]; the alternative would have widened a validated input boundary
per security-plan.md §Input Validation). **Lane modes:** `auto` = P-073/P-074/P-079, `drive+observe` =
P-067/P-072/P-075 — P-075 aggregates the four delegated timing budgets and three of those four (P-025, P-027,
P-037) are already `drive+observe` in the shipped table, so classifying the aggregate as `auto` would contradict
its own members.

The chunk introduces the classification and keeps every existing surface correct; the **visual treatment** of
not-Conductor's rows (distinct in the matrix, the footer roll-up and the CLI table) is the next working entry,
_Out-of-scope classification treatment_, and is deliberately not designed here.
