## Goal

Conductor's headline promise — "same scenario + seed ⇒ same emission stream shape" — is an architectural
property enforced by the `current_thread` runtime flavor and a single seeded `ChaCha8Rng`, not a test
convention (per architecture.md §Design Philosophy · §Established Decisions [Determinism RNG]). Today that
promise is frozen on disk for the **phase-transition** stream only. When
`2026-08-11-faithful-emission-dispatcher` landed the per-phase dispatcher, both committed goldens came back
byte-identical — correctly, because the pacing change preserved the transition stream exactly. That is the
gap this chunk closes: **the emission stream the dispatcher now produces is frozen nowhere**, so an
RNG/ordering/shape-mapping regression inside it would pass every gate.

The stream has two layers and they fail independently, so both are frozen (per test-plan §1 Coverage
triggers · property-test (determinism discipline) + §7 Golden artifacts). `conductor-timeline` produces
`EmissionPoint { phase_index, occurrence }` paced across each phase's own jittered gap — a pacing/ordering
regression lives here and never reaches the wire. `conductor-run::Dispatcher` maps that point onto a
`conductor-emit` primitive via `emission_seed(seed, phase_index, occurrence)`, a pure mix with no RNG state
of its own — a shape→primitive regression lives here and never moves the pacing. Each tier gets a committed
insta golden at two contrasting seeds (pinning seed-*sensitivity*, not just one shape, per `replay.rs`'s
precedent), plus a proptest generalizing replay determinism across the seed space. The dispatch-tier golden
is a **shape-projection** excluding `start/end_time_unix_nano`, which come from `SystemTime::now()` — the
seed governs identity, not the clock (per obs-plan §5 · the shipped `dispatch_wire.rs:357` precedent).

The chunk also discharges the **load-envelope close-out** the contract itself names, and P3 research
falsified its stated mechanism. `contracts/pulse-load-envelope.toml:40-42` predicted that asserting summed
emitting-phase duration would let both `[[exempt]]` entries retire on their own merits; measured across all
35 committed scenarios, both remain over the 600 000 ms ceiling on that basis, so the summed re-scope is a
no-op on the gate's verdict and retiring the exemptions under it turns the gate red. The envelope's own prose
bounds *sustained* storm, and summing three 5-minute bursts separated by 10-minute quiets is not sustained.
Asserting a **per-phase joint bound** instead — no single emitting phase over `max_sustained_storm_ms`, none
over `max_sustained_rate_spans_per_s` — puts every scenario inside (longest emitting phase 600 000 ms exactly
at the ceiling; highest rate 4.00 spans/s against 10 000), so the ledger genuinely retires to `[]` and the
contract's recorded end state becomes true. Operator-approved at P5 review; the correction and its evidence
are recorded in `scope.md` §PREMISE-CORRECTION and in the matrix `notes` for `v2-06`.
