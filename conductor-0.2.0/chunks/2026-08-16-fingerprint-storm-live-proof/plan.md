## Goal

The first **family live proof** of the version: drive `scenarios/fingerprint-storm.toml` end to end against a
live deterministic-L4 Pulse and assert P-017 (fingerprint identity), P-018 (storm cue thresholds) and P-074
(exactly-one-incident coalescing) on surfaces that actually carry the evidence. The predecessor chunks
cleared the path — the ingest→fingerprint gap was Conductor-side and is fixed, and `conductor preflight`
reached `ready:true` on 2026-08-16 — so this is the first chunk that can use that green gate for a family
rather than for the canary.

Before the leg can mean anything, the scenario has to be true. Research re-derived its arithmetic from
source and found the declared semantics broken in three ways: the header claims the identical/path/line
triple shares ONE fingerprint but the shipped blake3 derivation makes the path variant diverge (two
fingerprints, base ×12 and path ×6); neither phase boundary lands where its name claims (phase 1 ends with 4
base-fp occurrences, so the ≥5 Suggested cue never fires there); and the path fingerprint independently
clears ≥5 and raises a second Suggested cue that no document names. The fix, chosen by the operator at P4,
is the **product-faithful** one: `PathVariant` becomes an ABSOLUTE-path change, which Pulse's `normalize_frame`
strips, so the triple genuinely shares one fingerprint and every declared semantic is restored at once — 6
same-fp at the phase-1 boundary, 18 cumulative, one storm. Occurrence counts do not move, so all four
committed goldens stay byte-identical. A new `RelativePath` variant member carries the relative-path
significance the reshape retires, asserted at unit tier where `fingerprint-distinct.toml` already keeps the
type/frame half.

Identity cannot be graded through MCP read-back: `fingerprint_refs` is fed from the L4 model's
`evidence_refs`, pinned `[]` in deterministic mode, so the envelope's `fingerprints[]` is always empty on a
live leg. Identity and tier evidence therefore come from harvesting Pulse's own storm-detected log line
(the only one of its three carrying `fingerprint_hex`), via a **test-only** affordance rather than a shipped
seam — the live leg is an operator gate by construction and Conductor's product never needs to read Pulse's
logs. MCP still carries what it can: incident count and freshness for P-074's exactly-one claim. The leg
runs **both** family scenarios — `fingerprint-storm.toml` for the same-fingerprint half and
`fingerprint-distinct.toml` for the distinctness half `v2-11`'s acceptance also names — separated by more
than Pulse's 60 s retention window, because they share a base fingerprint and would otherwise contaminate
each other in either order. Per
architecture §Standard Contracts + §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency Posture], test-plan §6 and
§9, obs-plan §4 and §6, and security-plan §Input Validation + §Error Handling.

## Constraints & rejected approaches

- **Raising occurrence counts — REJECTED.** Breaks four committed snapshots across two crates and fixes
  neither defect: two fingerprints would remain and the path fingerprint would still raise its own cue.
- **Dropping `path` from the storm's mix — REJECTED (operator decision at P4).** Smallest diff and equally
  golden-free, but the storm would exercise no path variant, so P-017's path clause would be asserted only
  at unit tier and the scenario loses the triple it exists to teach.
- **A shipped log-harvest seam (in `conductor-verify` or a tenth crate) — REJECTED (operator decision at
  P4).** The live leg is an operator gate by construction; Conductor's product never needs to read Pulse's
  logs, and a shipped SUT-log reader would create a permanent artifact-hygiene surface for a one-directional
  proof. Test-only keeps the surface with the proof.
- **Do NOT assert a populated envelope `fingerprints[]` on the live leg.** `observation.fingerprints` is fed
  solely by `fingerprint_refs` (`extract.rs:114` → `lib.rs:413`/`:456`), which the deterministic-L4 chain
  pins empty. Asserting it would fail for a reason that is not Conductor's defect.
- **Do NOT silently fix a diverging read-back reader.** A key-set divergence is a recorded finding plus a
  stub test; readers degrade to empty rather than erroring, so a silent fix would hide a live contract break.
- **Do NOT raise a `cargo audit` floor, add a `deny.toml` ignore, or edit CI.** The fault is
  advisory-DATABASE class with nothing to raise to; the remedy is the bounded wait with `cargo deny`
  verified green.
- **Do NOT add a CI dependency on a live Pulse**, and do not treat a `Blocked` leg as a `Fail`.

## Implementation notes

- **Order matters at step 6.** The `p_ids` edit and the `UNBACKED_AUTO` removal must land together;
  committing either alone leaves `check_scenario_backing` red in the opposite direction.
- **The absolute-path prefix is load-bearing in three directions at once** — stripped by
  `is_absolute_path_start`, untouched by `redact_value`, non-host-revealing. A `C:\`-style or `/home/`-style
  prefix would be scrubbed by Conductor's own redaction and silently change the preimage.
- **`stacktrace_carries_no_absolute_host_path` asserts over `base()` only**, so it stays green — but the new
  test in step 4 is what actually guards the reshape.
- **Two frames, three normalized slots.** `base_exception()` carries 2 frames and `NORMALIZED_FRAMES = 3`, so
  both frames reach the preimage and the path reshape affects the whole identity.
- **The two live legs contaminate each other in EITHER order, and the fix is quiet time.** Both scenarios
  derive from the same module-level `base_exception()` (`dispatch.rs:205-214`), so `fingerprint-storm`'s
  base fingerprint and `fingerprint-distinct`'s `base-4x` fingerprint are **the same value**. Pulse retains
  occurrences for `DEFAULT_STORM_WINDOW_SECONDS = 60` (`storm.rs:63`), pruning on every `record_occurrence`
  and every heartbeat tick. Run distinct AFTER storm inside that window and its 4 base occurrences land on
  a fingerprint already carrying 18 — the `Absent "RetryStorm"` assertion fails for carry-over, not for a
  collapsed fingerprint. Run distinct FIRST and the storm's phase-1 boundary reads 10 instead of 6,
  breaking the P-018 tier-ladder assertion. **Separate the legs by more than 60 s of quiet** (each leg is
  only ~24 s and ~30 s), or restart against a fresh data dir between them. A failure here is an artifact of
  leg ordering, never evidence about Pulse.
- **Read the detector telemetry by the standing rules** — `tracked_fingerprints_count` reads 1 for a healthy
  identical-fp storm (never the occurrence count) and 0 on a late sample; `storms_detected_total` and
  `fingerprints_evicted_total` are the window-immune discriminators. Never read the gauge as evidence of
  absence.
- **The live-leg recipe is SIX items.** The working-route entry states five, but that text was authored
  2026-08-13; `.claude/rules/verification-harness.md:47` carries the accumulated chain and adds a **sixth**
  at 2026-08-14. Read the rules file, not the route entry — the route entry is the stale citation.
  - Item 3 is `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_MCP_ENABLED=true` in **Conductor's own** environment — inheritance is the
    sole channel (`spawn.rs` passes only the data dir via `.env(...)`), and without it every arm silently
    measures the read-back-unreachable path.
  - **Item 6 is `RUST_LOG=info,conductor_emit=debug`, and its FORM is load-bearing.** It surfaces the
    `emit.batch` wire-shape witness — the thing that tells you what actually reached the wire when a leg
    goes sideways, at zero cost when it doesn't. The **bare** per-target form (`conductor_emit=debug`)
    REPLACES the default filter rather than adding to it, blacking out every other target; measured across
    three forms, the bare one fails the CLI's own
    `agent_mode_routes_self_obs_to_the_log_file_not_stderr` while the paired one passes.
  - **Never set either form on an invocation that also runs the test suite** — the environment reaches the
    runner's child processes. This is why the scenario legs in Test Commands use the direct CLI invocation
    rather than `SCENARIO=… agent-run.sh run`, which bundles nextest + doctest + clippy ahead of the leg.
