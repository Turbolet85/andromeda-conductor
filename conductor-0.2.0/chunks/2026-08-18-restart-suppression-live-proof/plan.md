## Implementation notes
- **EWMA drift is the recurring hazard**: each burst raises the baseline the NEXT leg's magnitude is
  computed against (leg A measured 3.09-3.19 for a 3.5x intent). Hence the ascending-rate rule and the
  ≥3× margin; if a live leg silences a witness, tune the rate upward within the envelope — that is a
  recipe adjustment, not a premise failure. A mag that lands ≤10 on the relative-labeled leg degrades its
  label to `absolute_error_rate` — record honestly and re-tune.
- **Persistence is per-candidate and continuous** — one elevated identity crossing the 30s cutoff
  witnesses BOTH P-016 halves (drop-then-keep); windows are per-service and the whole scenario runs one
  service; the emitter drains RestartEvents on a 1s tick, so window opening skew is ≤1s.
- **Corpus-alive arithmetic**: conf = samples/100 capped at 1.0 → the sustained leg reaches Autonomous
  (incident forms, row stays non-Blocked) only if ~90+ samples accumulate before its ≥30s-persistence
  cues — size `baseline.occurrences` accordingly (candidate: 60). If the incident still auto-resolves
  before read-back (30s ticks; ~180s idle measured fatal), the row's honest Blocked is the budgeted
  fallback — never weaken the gate to avoid it.
- **P-015's "≤2s" SLO is Pulse-internal** (ingest-path detection): under declare-only, no Conductor row
  grades it; the harvest witnesses the event's existence + gap value, and whole-run `latency_ms` records
  against `<90s` (the v2-12 refined-precedent landing). The concretized v2-13 acceptance states this.
