## Goal

Re-drive `halo-hue-encoding` so it produces a stimulus of its own — an error stream sustained through Pulse's
incident formation — and grade what the delegated `metric.constellation.hue_update_ms` observable actually
measures. The prior chunk measured 35 581 ms and 36 705 ms against a 2 000 ms budget and proved the cause
structural: the observable reports staleness, and the scenario drove nothing, so both samples were the
preflight canary's (`v2-20` PREMISE-CORRECTION; `delegated_timing_harvest.rs:325-337`).

**Three terms of the fire site govern the design, and the third was found at this plan's review — it changes
the deliverable.** Research established the first two at SUT HEAD `83d4060`: the effect emits the **slowest**
changed dot's staleness and *that* dot's tier (`ConstellationCanvas.tsx:117-155`), and `visibleDots` drops any
service quiet longer than `LIVE_RECENCY_WINDOW_NANOS = 60 s` (`constellation-types.ts:27`). The third is
**quantization**: `ServiceRegistryEntry.last_seen_unix_nano` has **no ingest-path writer**. A sweep
(`grep -rn "last_seen_unix_nano\s*=[^=]" crates/ pulse-app/src` → 4 hits: `registry.rs:257` restart transition,
`registry.rs:336` tick, plus unrelated `buffer/src/drain.rs:492` and `corpus/src/contract.rs:546`) leaves the
steady-state writer as `registry.tick_all`, which sets the field to the **tick's** `now` and only when
`snapshot.current_quiet_duration_seconds == 0` — integer-truncated seconds since the floor's last observe
(`activity_floor.rs:142-149`). The tick is `DEFAULT_LIFECYCLE_HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL = Duration::from_secs(15)`
(`lifecycle/mod.rs:52`), passed unchanged at `main.rs:1489` with no env override.

Therefore `hue_update_ms ≈ t_sample − t_last_refreshing_tick`, distributed U(0, 15 s) at a randomly-timed
flip — **independent of the dispatch rate**, with roughly 13 % of flips landing under 2 s by coincidence.
**The ≤2 s bound is unmeasurable through this leaf while `last_seen` is lifecycle-tick-quantized**, and a
pass asserted against 2 000 ms would pin a tick coincidence as attainment. The chunk therefore ships the
**mechanism pin plus the measured value**, and no pass arm, until the SUT changes the writer. The SUT-side fix
(the registry copying the floor's `last_observed_unix_nanos`, or the fire site reading span arrival) is a
Pulse intake item, not this chunk's work (scope §2).

Attribution rides neither service identity (the leaf's allowlist is exactly `["duration_ms","severity_tier"]`
under the SUT's own "Aggregate-only: no service identifier", `observability.rs:986-990`) nor `severity_tier`
(a dot's tier is incident-derived, `registry.rs:59-65`, and incidents form only from Autonomous cues,
`coordinator.rs:395`, so the scenario must reach the same band the canary does). It is **temporal**, and the
canary is separated by construction: preflight readiness *requires* a fresh canary incident
(`canary.rs:1-7` — "asserts an incident opened AFTER that storm's emission instant"), so the canary's tier
flip and its hue sample land **before `scenario.run` starts**, and its dot leaves `visibleDots` 60 s after the
storm — before its incident auto-resolves. The chunk also discharges the scenario's unreachable declared tier
(CARRY §3.2) and the `ReadBack::AutoResolved` re-proof (CARRY §3.3).

## Constraints & rejected approaches

- **Asserting the ≤2 s bound as a pass — REJECTED, unmeasurable through this leaf.** `last_seen` is refreshed
  only at 15 s lifecycle ticks (§Goal), so a sample under 2 s is a tick coincidence (~13 % of flips), not
  attainment. No pass arm until the SUT changes the writer.
- **Raising the dispatch rate to make `last_seen` FRESH — REJECTED, it cannot.** The value is written by the
  tick, not by ingest, so no rate reaches the 2 s bound. (2/s is nonetheless CHOSEN in step 1 for a different
  reason: it makes every tick refresh by construction, which the mechanism pin depends on.)
- **Keeping 1/s and widening the pin to accept a `k × 15 s` offset — REJECTED as the weaker letter.** It
  would absorb the jitter beat into the assertion rather than removing its cause, and a pin that accepts an
  offset to "the preceding tick or the one before it" no longer distinguishes a refreshed tick from a skipped
  one — which is precisely the property under test.
- **A 60 s window offset to exclude the canary — REJECTED, it drops the subject.** Preflight readiness
  requires a *fresh* canary incident (`canary.rs:1-7`), so the canary's flip precedes `scenario.run`; the
  subject's own flip lands at phase-2 start + ~27…67 s (≈5 s to reach 10 identical fingerprints at 2/s in the
  60 s storm window, `storm.rs:65`/`:80`; + the 20–60 s L3 digest cadence,
  `contracts/pulse-run-contract.toml:31`; + ~2 s canned L4), so a +60 s edge would discard most of that range
  as a spurious absence-`Err`.
- **Taking phase boundaries from `runs/<run_id>.jsonl` — REJECTED, it carries no phase data.** The frozen
  per-leg `live-suite/{label}.jsonl` does.
- **Discriminating the sample by `severity_tier` or by service identity — REJECTED, both structurally
  unavailable** (§Goal).
- **Reusing `grade()`'s worst-wins fold for the re-driven bound — REJECTED.** It would grade the canary's
  sample; the other three bounds keep it unchanged.
- **Adding an `[[expected]]` check or a `budget_ms` for the hue bound — REJECTED.** Those grade Conductor's
  own MCP round-trip; binding a Pulse-internal duration to them is the category error `v2-20`'s notes record.
- **An `[[exempt]]` entry to fit the sustained window — REJECTED and unnecessary, at 2/s as at 1/s.** Longest
  emitting phase 150 000 ms against `max_sustained_storm_ms = 600 000`; rate term `300 × 1 × 1000` against
