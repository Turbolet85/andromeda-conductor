## Goal

Vary the **WebView2 runtime major** on the hosted `windows-2025` runner and re-observe probe (a). This is
the one variable every passing case shares and the single failing case lacks: passing = {runtime
152.0.4191.x × driver 151, 2026-09-02 morning} ∪ {runtime 152.0.4191.x × driver 152.0.4191.53, since
2026-09-02 14:45}; failing = {runtime 151.0.4129.101 × driver 151.0.4129.101} on the runner. The prior
chunk established, with **no driver in the picture at all**, that the runner's runtime never opens its
remote-debugging endpoint — so every driver-layer hypothesis is retired and the runtime is the variable
that was never varied.

The work is one new gate step in the `a11y` job that installs the signed Evergreen bootstrapper with its
Authenticode signature checked **before** it executes, plus one line that stages an existing but
uncaptured liveness witness. Per security-plan §Security Anti-Patterns → Universal the signature check is a
terminal gate, never an advisory line; per arch §Occupied Resources (`CONDUCTOR_A11Y_STRICT`), test-plan §9
(E2E webview row), obs-plan §11 (CI) and a11y-plan §11 (Anti-Patterns → CI) no gate step may gain
`continue-on-error` — research confirmed that boundary is a **preserve**, not a change: the three existing
`continue-on-error` sites are the two diagnostics and the diag upload, none on a gate path.

This chunk is a **probe, and both outcomes complete it**. If the endpoint opens on 152+, the runtime major
was the cause. If it still never opens, the runtime-major hypothesis is **falsified** and that is a real
measurement to record with its mechanism — per this project's standing rule a disproved premise is
recorded, never hidden and never quietly relaxed. The remaining unmeasured candidates (no interactive
desktop session on a hosted runner; a Session-0 / service-account restriction on the WebView2 browser
process; an image policy on remote debugging) then become the surviving set.

   `[precondition] <NAME>: <state>` idiom already used at the routine arm — and `exit 1`. No
   `-ErrorAction SilentlyContinue` may wrap the signature evaluation. This is the never-silently-downgrade
   discipline (security-plan §Security Anti-Patterns → Universal): a warning that proceeds is the exact
   banned shape.
