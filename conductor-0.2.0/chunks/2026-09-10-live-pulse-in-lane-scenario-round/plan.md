## Goal

Drive the three committed in-lane scenarios — `live-only-service-truth` (P-067),
`investigate-actions-functional` (P-072) and `constellation-severity-live-wiring` (P-079) — against a
live, operator-launched Pulse, and produce for each a **non-blocked verdict via MCP read-back with
journal and `runs.db` evidence**, which is exactly `verification-matrix.json#v2-04`'s acceptance. This
is a **round, not harness work**: research settled that no source delta is required (`research.md`
§Files to modify), so the deliverable is driven evidence plus the recorded disposition of one finding.

The round's difficulty is not the driving — it is **sequencing**. Every `conductor run` fires its own
preflight canary, and Pulse dedupes a new incident against **any open incident, not by fingerprint**
(`verification-harness.md:50`, measured). Three legs back-to-back therefore land two
`Blocked: no incident opened after the canary storm was emitted` rows — precisely the state `v2-04`
forbids. Each leg is consequently fired behind a **≥150 s quiet window** (Pulse's 120 s idle plus a full
30 s resolver tick), the same interval the shipped `live_suite()` already uses at `agent-run.sh:127`.

Grading is settled and differs per scenario, which is why each leg is planned for its own outcome rather
than one shared expectation (per arch §Standard Contracts + §Established Decisions [Read-Back Dependency
Posture]). P-067 and P-072 carry **no `[[expected]]` block**, so they route through the
operator-checklist branch to `manual_record` → `ManualCheck`, or to `KnownResidual` when the read-back is
empty or degraded — **both non-blocked**. P-079 carries a Hard `CountAtLeast ≥ 1` and grades
programmatically; its floor reads `span_refs`, which Pulse writes empty at every production incident, so
the floor is unmet and routes to `CalibrationRegion` **regardless of the declared `Hard`** — also
non-blocked, and an already-ratified treatment (see Constraints).
