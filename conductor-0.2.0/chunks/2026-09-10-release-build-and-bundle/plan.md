## Goal

Close `conductor-0.2.0`. Produce the release binary and the Tauri 2 bundle behind the standing supply-chain
gate, record a final SLO verification pass against the **shipped** artifact, and claim the version's last two
capabilities (`v2-27`, `v2-21`) so the matrix done-test flips.

The release gate is the headless binary; the bundle is convenience, not a gate (per architecture.md
§Established Decisions [Deployment]). The gate ordering is not cosmetic — `cargo audit` and `cargo deny`
green over an un-drifted `Cargo.lock` are a **precondition of** `cargo build --release`, never a follow-up
(per security-plan §Security Anti-Patterns → Universal; test-plan §11 → CI). Two build facts bind: the
webview bundle must exist before any cargo compile of `conductor-tauri` (`generate_context!` resolves
`build.frontendDist` at compile time), and the **`custom-protocol` feature, not the profile**, decides whether
the binary serves the embedded bundle or opens `devUrl` (per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns — Build
system).

The chunk also lands the two open carried defects. **CARRY A** — the process-global panic-hook race in
`conductor-core/src/obs.rs` — is fixed at the cause with one shared in-code guard, which the code-graph
bounds to exactly two tests. **CARRY C** — the SR leg's run-to-run announcement variance — is a bounded
cause investigation whose remainder routes to residuals; per a11y-plan §3/§11 the SR leg is supplemental,
never sole, so no release claim rests on it either way.

Three operator decisions were taken at the P4 review round and are binding here: the version stamp **bumps
0.1.0 → 0.2.0**; the SLO pass drives **bounded in-lane live legs** through the shipped binary; **CARRY C
stays in this chunk** as a bounded investigation.

- **Serializing CARRY A's tests with `--test-threads=1` or a nextest profile knob — REJECTED.** test-plan

- **Moving the two panic-hook tests into their own `tests/*.rs` binary — REJECTED.** It would require making
  `build_subscriber` and `log_panic` `pub` (an integration test binary is an external crate, so `pub(crate)`

  Decisions [Module Boundaries].
- **Re-running the `sr` leg until `S3-04` announces — REJECTED.** a11y-plan §11 SLO and test-plan §10 both
  ban a retry policy; §11 → E2E bans an in-test `sleep(N)` to synchronise, a carve-out already proposed and
  refused at `2026-09-06-operator-gated-live-suite`.
- **Adding `@tauri-apps/cli` to the frontend tree — REJECTED.** It would pull the bundler CLI into
  `package-lock.json` and the npm audit arm for no gain over the established `cargo install` host-tool
  pattern.
- **Re-declaring the mis-tiered scenarios in this chunk — REJECTED.** Scenario calibration is not release
  work; the finding is recorded and routed (see below), and P-079's own residual already targets `next`.
- **Driving the composed `run --live` suite as the SLO pass — REJECTED** (operator decision at the P4
  review): test-plan records its auto-resolve leg as not run-stable even under the stretched bootstrap

- **Promoting `cargo auditable`, release signing or an SBOM step into a gate — REJECTED.** security-plan
