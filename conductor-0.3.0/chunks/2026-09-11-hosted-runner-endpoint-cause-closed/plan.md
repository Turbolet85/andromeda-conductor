**Arm A — the module-version reading, taken where its subject is alive.** The probe is correct; its POSITION
made the reading unreachable. Section (2) of `scripts/webview2-cause-probe.ps1` enumerates loaded modules of
`msedgewebview2` / `conductor-tauri` / `msedge`, but the step that invokes it runs *after* the

measured at run `34586959536`. The only window in the whole job where those processes exist is probe (a) of

`tasklist` inside that window shows the app and three webview children alive. The fix is **placement**, not a
longer timeout: a timeout cannot recover a process that no longer exists.

**Arm B — elevation varied, on the side where the control already exists.** The predecessor named elevation as
the one surviving difference (`IsElevatedAdmin: True` on the runner, `False` on the dev host) and recorded it
as *a difference, not a demonstrated cause* — no probe varied it. Varying it on the runner is the expensive
direction: a hosted step is elevated by construction, so de-elevating means a scheduled-task or `runas` token
dance whose own failure modes would then have to be separated from the endpoint's. The inverse is one run:
elevate the **dev host**, where the endpoint works and the non-elevated control is already committed (routine
arm at 12 passing / 2 skipped, banner `[webview2 152.0.4191.66 windows]`, 2026-09-10), and re-run the routine
arm there. This is the discipline the earlier raw-`msedgedriver` probe broke — a probe that has not produced
the KNOWN-GOOD outcome on the known-good host discriminates nothing; here that outcome is already on record,
from this host, under the one variable being changed.

Both outcomes are deliverables: if `DevToolsActivePort` disappears under local elevation, elevation is
established with a control on both sides and no CI round-trip; if it survives, elevation is retired **as
measured on this host** — and only then does a runner-side arm earn its cost. Per architecture.md §Established
Decisions [CI/CD] + a11y-plan §9 Platform + test-plan §6, neither result may be written wider than the host it
was taken on.

**Arm C — driver/runtime major skew, varied (added 2026-09-11 on the operator's ruling, ahead of arm B).**

`msedgedriver: 152.0.4191.66`, `Edge browser: 152.0.4191.66`, WebView2 runtime `152.0.4191.66` before the

The arm that varies it is one conditional inside an existing step, not a deletion. The step's name states a

## Constraints & rejected approaches

- **De-elevating on the RUNNER — rejected.** A hosted step is elevated by construction; de-elevating inside it
  needs a scheduled-task or `runas` token dance whose own failure modes would then have to be separated from
  the endpoint's. It earns its cost only if arm B leaves elevation standing.
- **Reading modules in probe (b)'s driver-launched host as well — rejected** (research §Open questions). Probe
  (b) is where the failure occurs, but its process exists only transiently inside a POST that fails, and the
  isolation step's own comment records that msedgedriver REMOVES its scoped profile dir when a session fails —
  so the read is a race, and a race that returns nothing is indistinguishable from the gap this chunk is
  closing. Probe (a)'s window is deterministic and is the one the directive's own 0.645 s measurement names.
- **An inline probe body in `ci.yml`, or a second script — rejected.** Arch registers ONE probe file; a
  duplicate silently forks the dev-host control from the CI reading, which is the whole reason the body lives
  in a repo script.
- **A longer timeout on the existing cause-probe step — rejected.** The subject is a process that no longer
  exists; no timeout recovers it. The defect is placement.
- **Changing the existing `WebView2 cause probes (diagnostic)` step — rejected.** It keeps running all three
  sections; its `no live process` line for section (2) is now expected and is explained in the reading, not
  suppressed.
- **Do not ask the skill to commit.** Commit and push are operator steps (step 6), placed before any entry
  consuming a CI run.
- **Do not force the `v3-01` claim.** The capability is claimed by this chunk, but whether its acceptance is
  MET depends on arm B's outcome, which was unknown at review time. If elevation is established as the cause,
  the decision is reached and the claim discharges. If elevation SURVIVES and the four named candidates are
  exhausted without the readings supporting a remediable-vs-permanent decision, that clause is **not met** —
  record the measurement in `evidence/reading.md`, SURFACE it, and let the capability return to the pool with a
  `notes` line, exactly as the predecessor did rather than weakening the clause. A claim discharged by wording
  is the failure mode this version exists to retire.
