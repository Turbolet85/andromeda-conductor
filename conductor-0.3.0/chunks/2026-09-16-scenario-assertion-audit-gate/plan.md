## Goal

Epoch 2 established two corpus properties by hand and recorded them in prose: every committed
`[[expected]]` assertion is satisfiable or retired (`v3-04`), and every scenario's declared `slo_tier`
is accounted for against its own summed phase duration (`v3-05`). Both are TRUE at HEAD and both are
currently re-establishable only by repeating the derivation. This chunk makes them **mechanical and
re-runnable by a reader who did not write them**, which is `verification-matrix.json#v3-06`.

The design is the shipped exact-set ledger gate, not a new invention. `check_load_envelope`
(`load_envelope.rs:367-398`) already grades a committed `Vec<Exemption>` against a measured set in
BOTH directions — failing on `unpinned` (a new member outside the pin), `rotted` (a pinned member that
no longer qualifies) and `lost_subject` (a pin naming a scenario that no longer exists) — and
`testing.md:66` states why that shape is right here: it records work owed at full precision and can
only shrink under compulsion, rather than weakening an assertion. A new `check_scenario_audit` in
`conductor-core` grades a new committed ledger the same way, and a three-armed crate-local test over
the REAL committed corpus registers it as a gate that fails the build (per test-plan §6's
`coverage_gate.rs` precedent; arch §Established Decisions [Accepted Capability Set] for the
named-`CoreError` failure class).

Two research findings shape what the ledger holds. First, the **closed-set property needs no probe**:
`SloTier` (`scenario.rs:50-62`) is a plain derived `Deserialize` with three renamed variants and no
`#[serde(other)]`, so a fourth tier cannot survive `from_toml_str` — the property is enforced by
construction, and the gate's job is to assert that the type stays closed, not to re-scan for
violations. Second, the **two surviving assertions are satisfiable but non-discriminating**: measured
at SUT HEAD `83d4060`, deterministic mode returns a fixed L4 payload regardless of prompt
(`deterministic_inference.rs:126`) whose pinned `"severity": "autonomous"` maps through
`map_l4_incident_severity` (`inference_runtime.rs:697-703`) to `IncidentSeverity::Error`, rendering
`severity_label` `"error"` (`markdown.rs:359-366`) into every report — so both `Contains "error"`
blocks pass on a token their own stimulus never produced. A static check cannot grade discrimination
(test-plan §11 routes SUT-behaviour claims to the live gate), so the gate asserts **satisfiability**
and the ledger **records the weakness with its reason**, exact-set in both directions. The finding is
thereby enforced by something rather than by memory, which is the capability's own standard.
