## Constraints & rejected approaches

- **`new Set(names).size` against `focusableCount` — REJECTED, measured wrong.** The idle console has six
  focusable elements but only FIVE distinct accessible names: `CoverageMatrix.tsx:60` and `RunReport.tsx:47`
  are both unnamed `<div tabIndex={0}>` scroll regions and `activeName()` returns `el.tagName` for each, so
  both project to `DIV`. The comparison yields 5 against 6 and leaves the assertion red. Confirmed in the
  baseline log's own bracket list.
- **Giving the two scroll regions `aria-label`s to break the collision — REJECTED.** Both carry a comment
  recording that the name deliberately sits on the inner `<table>`, because a named focusable region made
  NVDA read the whole table in one utterance (SR rows S0-09 / E0-05). The assertion follows the design.
- **Fixing `:384` only and leaving `:397` — REJECTED on measurement.** They share one root cause; `:397`
  passed on runtime 131 by walk-start luck and would ship as a latent flake into the configuration this
  chunk claims green.
- **Adding or removing a control, or a `tabindex`, to reconcile a count — BANNED** (design-system
