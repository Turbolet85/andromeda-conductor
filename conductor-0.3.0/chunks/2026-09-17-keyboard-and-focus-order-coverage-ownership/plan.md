## Goal

Every keyboard-reachability and focus-order claim `a11y-plan.md` §5 makes gets exactly one stated owner, and
the statement becomes **mechanically enforced rather than prose-only**. The chunk commits a claim→owner
enumeration beside the suites and a checker that holds it to reality: each named owner's spec must exist and
must actually assert something, so a spec that names a claim in its title and asserts nothing can never stand
as an owner. This is architecture's standing shape for a coverage claim — a statement plus a gate holding it to
exact-set equality, as `check_sut_drift` and `check_scenario_backing` already do (per architecture §Established
Decisions [Accepted Capability Set]).

The hold-dependent half — trap entry/containment and focus restoration — is owned by the driven arm
(`operator-hold.e2e.ts`, `npm run a11y:driven`), which CI structurally cannot run because it needs a live
preflight-ready Pulse (per test-plan §9/§11 + architecture §Established Decisions [CI/CD]). Its CI carve-out is
stated rather than left implicit, naming what gates in its place, with its configuration rather than as a bare
verdict (per a11y-plan §11 Strategy + §3 Configuration).

The `a11y-plan.md` prose itself is **not** a touchpoint — it moves through wrap's amendment flow (per
`plan-template.md` §Discipline). Operator decision recorded 2026-09-17: the enumeration is /implement's, the

1. **Commit the claim→owner enumeration** at `crates/conductor-tauri/ui/test/a11y/claim-ownership.ts`. One row
   per `a11y-plan.md` §5 keyboard/focus claim, keyed by the bullet's **stable subject identity** (e.g.
   `run-console-HOLD focus trap`), never by a line coordinate — a11y-plan §3 *Configuration* requires naming by

   Each row carries exactly one of three states:
