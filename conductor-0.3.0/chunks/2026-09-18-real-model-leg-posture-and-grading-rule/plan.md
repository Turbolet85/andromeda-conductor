## Goal

Ship the committed, dated statement that fixes — **before any real-model leg is driven** — the launch posture,
the grading rule and the per-leg quiet window under which a leg with Pulse's deterministic L4 mode OFF will be
run and judged. This is the posture half of Epoch 4's pair; the drive is the next route entry's work. The
separation is mandated, not stylistic: `verification-matrix.json#v3-09`'s route note states that a chunk which
drives first and decides the grading afterwards "satisfies the letter and defeats the capability".

P3 turned the posture from a formality into a substantive finding. Tracing the hypothesis observable to its
writer in the SUT's own source established that **a single-storm real-model leg has no hypothesis observable at
all**: `retrieve_report` renders its `## Hypotheses` section only when `incident.resolution_summary_text` parses
as an `L4Output`, and neither writer of that field fires on first incident creation — the live writer fires only
on a dedupe re-generation of an already-active incident, the other only after resolution. A leg designed the
obvious way would read back a degraded report, grade nothing, and look like a model failure. The posture's job is
to state the emission profile that makes the observable exist, alongside the launch terms and the grading rule
(per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Probabilistic-Assertion Policy] + test-plan §9/§11 + obs-plan §3/§10).

It also discharges the standing **PREREQ**: `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` has deferred
twice consecutively and the third consecutive re-pin trips the age trigger, so this chunk runs it.
