Run a third pre-registered real-model series for `v3-09` against a Pulse that carries its incident-surfacing fix
(`a2addb3`: the L4 schema asks for the decision after the analysis, the storm digest's OVERALL line no longer
reads `nominal`, and a no-incident outcome is logged with its reason). The 2026-09-30 series graded none of its
three drives, because the real model dismissed the canary's storms in d1 and the scenario's own storm digest in
d2 and d3. This series measures whether the fix changes that live. It holds the same discipline: the design and
the rule are fixed in the posture contract before d1, the captures are digest-pinned, and nothing is retried until
it passes (per test-plan §6 Real-model interpretation leg; §9 Live-Pulse scenarios; architecture §Occupied
Resources, the posture-contract entry; §Established Decisions [Probabilistic-Assertion Policy]).

One hermetic change lands first, so a dismissal is recorded with Pulse's OWN stated cause instead of an inferred
one. At `a2addb3` Pulse logs `interpretation.incident.skipped` with closed-enum fields (`skip_reason`, `decision`,
`severity`, `digest_kind`), all four allowlisted. The capture prints that line, and each `canary:` line carries its
paired inference's `skip_reason`. Both changes sit OUTSIDE the grading rule, which stays byte-identical, because
every prior series holds its recorded rule equal to the current one (research.md §Files inspected). Pulse's line
reaches committed evidence through the existing source (2) of the capture ingest row, fields-only, through the
four-stage scrub chain. That is not a widening (security-plan §Input Validation, the real-model capture ingest row).

The rest is gated on two operator slots: building Pulse's binaries in the Pulse tree, and the model runs on `:4317`.
`pulse-app` and the MCP sidecar are both rebuilt from `a2addb3` and each is proven. `pulse-app` is proven by
content: strings the fix introduces are present, and the string it retired is absent. The sidecar has no
source-determinable new string, so it is measured first and falls back to build provenance (scope §What this chunk
builds item 2). The series is three drives on ONE fresh letters-only data dir. Its verdict is stated by a harvest
test over the pinned captures. The `v3-09` ref test is written ONLY if the pass condition holds.
