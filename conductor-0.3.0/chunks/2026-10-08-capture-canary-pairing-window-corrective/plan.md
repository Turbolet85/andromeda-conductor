## Goal
The real-model capture prints one `canary:` line per retry-storm digest of the preflight canary. Today it hands
its pairing only the Pulse lines stamped before the scenario's emission instant, so a canary digest whose tick
lands a few milliseconds before the instant, with its prompt assembly a millisecond after it, prints
`pipeline-fault` with `parse=none` whatever Pulse did. That happened on d1 and d2 of the 2026-10-07 sixth series
(`scope.md`, Causal claims; the series' `evidence/attempt-ledger.md:251-276`). The founder ruled it fixed in 0.3.0
as a small separate chunk (`inputs#I1` §1).

After this chunk the capture still SELECTS a digest by its tick's stamp (strictly before the instant), and the
pairing of a selected digest reads its inference wherever those lines are stamped. The scenario's own storm digest
still never prints as a canary. The proof is a default-suite test set over in-test lines built from the recorded
stamps, with a failing-first measurement. No live drive, no GPU, no committed capture or pin touched, the decision
rule and the six digest-pinned contract sections byte for byte (per test-plan §6 Scenario: Fingerprint-storm,
Real-model interpretation leg; obs-plan §4 Real-model posture; security-plan §Security Anti-Patterns → Data
Protection).

### The three questions the relay asks (`inputs#I1` §3)
1. **What the classifier prints, and how the scenario's digest is told apart.** For a canary storm whose tick
   precedes the instant by milliseconds it prints `canary: surfaced … parse=ok created=false deduped=true
   skip_reason=none`, which is what Pulse's log bears out on d1 and d2. The scenario's own digest is told apart by
   the SIGN of its tick's stamp against the instant, never by distance and never by a digest identity, which
   Pulse's lines do not carry. The instant is the `timeline.execute` span's open, taken before the scenario's first
   emission (`crates/conductor-timeline/src/scheduler.rs:112-116`), and a tier-1 `retry_storm` digest needs the
   Autonomous band, which the scenario's storm reached 5002, 5001 and 5000 ms after the instant on the three
   drives. Its tick is therefore stamped after the instant: it is never selected, and as the next retry-storm tick
   it closes the last canary's segment. In d1 the canary's prompt is 06:32:46.356Z and the scenario's is
   06:32:52.168Z; the canary's own dedupe line carries that same 06:32:52.168Z stamp and sits two lines earlier in
   the file, which is why the pairing reads file order. The rule errs one way only: a canary tick stamped at or
   after the instant (d3, 11 ms after) gets no line, as today.
2. **The failing-first control.** Step 4 runs the new d1- and d2-shaped tests against the pre-fix composition and
   records that each reads `pipeline-fault` with `parse=none`. A standing test keeps that reading reproducible
   after the fix.
3. **Architecture's registries are not grown.** This chunk expects no architecture amendment: no architecture
   text describes the pairing window (research.md §Sweeps; the sixth series' architecture entry says so in its
   Kept line). §Occupied Resources stands at 38114 B and §Established Decisions at 38082 B of 38115 B; if a wrap
   detector proposes an amendment to either, it frees bytes there first.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- Handing the pairing the whole window with no selection — rejected: measured on the replay, it prints the
  scenario's own digest as a fourth `canary:` line on every drive and moves the closing count (d2: 0 → 3).
- Telling a late canary tick from the scenario's by the storm line's fingerprint, to recover the d3 shape —
  rejected: it is a second order-based inference, it is wider than the pick, and d3 is named as a two-line control
  (`inputs#I1` §2).
- A cut position in the file instead of each tick's own stamp — rejected: d1's window holds a stamp inversion
  between adjacent lines (1 of 6200 pairs, `pairing-probe.txt`).
- A committed fixture file, or a line copied from a committed capture or from Pulse's log — never: the shapes are
  built in the test from stamps and line kinds (security-plan §Security Anti-Patterns → Data Protection).
- Editing a committed capture, a digest pin, a measured table, `real_model_harvest.rs` or any pinned contract
  section — never. The sixth series' table keeps d1 and d2 with one `pipeline_fault` token, as printed.
- A live drive, a `pulse-app` launch or a replay of the capture binary over a series' data dir — not planned. If
  implement finds that only a live drive can prove a step, it stops and reports (`inputs#I2`).
- A test that parses the posture contract, or any new read of an environment handle — never.
- A new test target, grading module, dependency, span, log line or envelope key — never.
- Changing the canary's storm count, gap or size, or when the emission instant is taken — never: the race itself
  is not moved.
